Closes #4030.

Add RingCard to Ouro HX for web: caller context, equal call choices, in-place connecting and recovery, and persistent ring and failure announcements.[^ring]

Answer and decline are person-only Actions. Page-owned shortcuts remain the page's job.[^ax]

The card leaves existing focus alone, hands focus onward when recovery disappears, and retains height after content wraps on resize.[^focus]

Use `arriving` for a newly received call; a restored call is visible immediately. Height observation does not force layout during mount.[^arrival]

Public demo: https://ui.ouroboros.bot/docs/ring-card/

Synthetic-data captures from the production docs build, not a calling-service integration. Look at the equal, quiet choices and the single in-place audio recovery.[^ring]
