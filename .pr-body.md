Small additions an app asked for. This pull request is stacked on the one that adds Message's menu and ThreadSummary: [Message chat layouts](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/pull/289).

- `Menu`'s `items` entries take an `icon`, drawn before the label at 16px and left out of the item's name.[^icon] `Message`'s `menu` passes its entries to Menu, so its items show icons too. See [Menu](https://ui.ouro.bot/docs/menu/) and [Message](https://ui.ouro.bot/docs/message/).
- `ThreadSummary` takes `label`, its button's accessible name, so a post's thread button can say "2 replies to Riley's post Settings mockups" while it shows "2 replies".[^label] A label that does not start with the button's words says so in development, following WCAG's [label in name](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/label-in-name). See [ThreadSummary](https://ui.ouro.bot/docs/thread-summary/).

Stories, agent-type snippets and changes fragments are updated.
