Reported by the consuming app (its issue 3028), measured in Chromium at 4x CPU while typing in a chat view with a message arriving every 400 ms, over about 6 s of typing.
Not reproduced in this repository: the app's profile is private, and these are the figures it reported.

Before (group context value rebuilt on every render):
  context propagation calls: 4300 to 10000, walking 0.7 to 1.3 million fibers
  key-to-paint p95: 44 ms
After (context value memoized by content, onLayout read through a ref):
  context propagation calls: 0
  key-to-paint p95: 35 ms
