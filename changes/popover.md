- New: `Popover`, `Tooltip` and `HoverCard`, portalled at `--ouro-layer` like Dialog and Sheet. `Popover` takes `trigger`, or `open` with `onOpenChange` and `anchor`; a required `title` (`hideTitle`); `side` and `align`; and `presentation="auto"`, which makes it a bottom sheet below 640px. `Tooltip` takes `content` with its trigger as the child, never opens after a touch, and warns in development when its content holds essential information. `HoverCard` previews a link on devices that can hover, and nowhere else.
- `OuroProvider` now groups tooltips (Base UI's `Tooltip.Provider`): once one shows, its neighbours open instantly. `ouro-lib` therefore depends on `@base-ui/react`, which every component already installs.
