- New: `Toolbar` takes `floating`, which draws the bar as glass over content, such as a calling app's controls over video: translucent with a blur, a hairline, a soft shadow and rounded corners from the theme, solid with no blur under reduced transparency and solid with a defined border under more contrast; where it floats stays the app's layout ([docs](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/docs/Toolbar.md), [test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/toolbar/toolbar.floating.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/toolbar/toolbar.floating.test.tsx::Toolbar floating > turns solid with no blur when the system asks for reduced transparency)")).
- New: `Toolbar` takes `autoHide={ms}`, which fades the bar out with opacity after the pointer rests over the element it is placed in, and brings it back on a pointer move there or any key; it never fades while the pointer is over it, focus is inside it, a menu, popover or tooltip from it is open, or after a key until the pointer moves; while faded it is `aria-hidden`, cannot be focused and drops its blur, with `data-hidden` on the root and `onHiddenChange` for the app's layout ([docs](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/docs/Toolbar.md), [test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/toolbar/toolbar.floating.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/toolbar/toolbar.floating.test.tsx::Toolbar autoHide > is hidden from screen readers and unfocusable while faded, and Tab brings it back first)")).
