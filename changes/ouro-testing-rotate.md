- The `ouro-testing` kit adds `rotate(width, height)` (testing/rotate.ts), which resizes the viewport and then resets the page zoom that Chromium's phone emulation can leave after a rotation, and the `pageZoom`, `resetPageZoom` and `setPageZoom` commands. `rotate` throws, naming the test, if something else had zoomed the page, after still resizing and resetting the zoom. The `ouro-touch` project now fails any test that leaves the page zoomed, naming it.
