- New: `Emoji` and `EmojiText`, an image emoji catalogue's emoji: `Emoji` draws one as an `<img>` named by its name, an animated one playing while its button is under the mouse or in focus and still with reduced motion, and `EmojiText` draws a message's `:shortcode:` tokens and emoji text as the catalogue's images. `EmojiItem` and `EmojiCatalogue` describe the catalogue: a standard set and an organization's custom emoji.
