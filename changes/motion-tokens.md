- Changed: motion tokens. New `--ouro-ease-out`, `--ouro-ease-in-out`, `--ouro-ease-drawer`, `--ouro-duration-press` and `--ouro-duration-sheet`; `--ouro-duration-fast` is now 150ms (was 100ms) and `--ouro-duration-slow` 300ms (was 400ms); the `press` utility scales in and out in 160ms instead of a 750ms spring; popovers, hover cards and tooltips open in 200ms on the ease-out token and no longer use `ease-in`; a linked Item, Card or Table row highlights on hover only for a fine pointer.
