- New: `Item` and `ItemGroup`. `Item` is a list row with `media`, `title`, `description` and `actions`; with `href` the whole row is one link, at least 44px tall on a touch screen, and every control inside stays its own tab stop and takes its own clicks and taps. `stackBelow="sm"` drops the actions under the text on a phone, and `mediaAbove="sm"` puts the photo full width above the text there, as a recipe list does. `ItemGroup` makes the rows a list named by its `label`, with lines between them and an `empty` message.
