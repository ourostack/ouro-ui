- New: `Toaster` and `toast()`. Render `<Toaster />` once, then call `toast("Saved", { tone, description, action, duration })` from anywhere (`toast` is in `lib/ouro/toast`, installed with every component). Up to three toasts show; later ones wait their turn, and each one's countdown starts when it shows. Toasts pause on hover and focus, dismiss with a swipe, and are announced once: politely, or assertively for `destructive`. A toast with an `action` stays until it is dismissed unless you pass a `duration`.
- `ouro-base` adds the `ouro-progress` and `ouro-shimmer` keyframes. The `ouro-testing` kit adds a `touchDrag` command for real touch swipes in Chromium.
