- The test kit's `expectMotion` (`lib/ouro/testing/motion.ts`) no longer fails when the browser restarts a transition or CSS animation and the restarted one runs its whole course between two frames, as it can on a slow frame. It listens for the part's transition and animation events while it waits and accepts a replacement only for the same property (an animation of the same name) on the same element that began after the cancellation and ended without being cancelled itself; a transition cancelled with nothing, or with another property, in its place still fails.
