- New: `SwipeActions`, a list row (wrap an `Item`) whose `leading` and `trailing` actions a horizontal swipe reveals, with touch or pen. The first action of a side is its primary one, and a swipe past 60% of the row runs it on release, so pair a destructive one with a toast Undo. Every action is also in the row's actions button, a `Menu` named with `label` (`actionsLabel`, default "Actions for {label}"), which shows with a mouse and is kept for the keyboard and screen readers on a touch screen. A vertical swipe on a row scrolls the page, a linked row follows its link on a tap and never on a swipe, one row is open at a time, and an open row closes on a tap outside it or on it, on scroll and on Escape. The sides follow the reading direction, and with reduced motion the row jumps open and its actions fade in.
