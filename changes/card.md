- New: `Card`, a surface with `title`, `description`, `action`, `media`, a body and `footer`. With `href` the whole card is one link with one tab stop, named by its title, and every control inside (a share button over the cover, a link in the body, a button in `action` or `footer`) stays its own tab stop and takes its own clicks and taps. `stackBelow="sm"` drops the action under the title on a phone.
