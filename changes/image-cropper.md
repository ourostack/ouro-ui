- New: `ImageCropper`, a dialog that crops a photo to a rectangle of `aspect` or a circle: drag or the arrow keys move it the way they point, the slider or Plus and Minus zoom it, and Save hands the cropped image to `onCrop` as a `File`. When the browser cannot open the image or save the crop, it shows and announces why (`labels.openError`, `labels.saveError`) and calls `onError` with `"open"` or `"save"`.
