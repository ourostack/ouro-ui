- New: `Inspector` (`@group App setup`), X-ray for a page built with Ouro HX. While `open`, it outlines every Ouro component with a tag naming its registry item (a link to its docs: Ouro's public docs, or `docsHref(item)`), follows them through scrolling, resizing, overlays and page changes (measured at most once a frame), and shows a bar whose list (a `Sheet`) names every component in document order with Docs and, from `sourceHref(item, element)`, Source links for keyboard and screen reader users. Outlines never take a pointer, so the page stays usable; Escape or Close calls `onOpenChange(false)`. Its own controls carry `data-ouro-control="inspector"`, so an app's census of its own controls can leave them out.
