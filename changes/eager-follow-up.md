- EmojiPicker: [with a catalogue, the quick row's and the grid's first eight rows' images are fetched at high priority and the rest at low, in order](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/emoji-picker/emoji-picker.catalogue.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/emoji-picker/emoji-picker.catalogue.test.tsx::EmojiPicker with a catalogue > fetches every image as it opens, the quick row and the grid's first eight rows first, then the rest in order)"); [while the browser asks to save data, the rest wait until they near the screen](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/emoji-picker/emoji-picker.catalogue.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/emoji-picker/emoji-picker.catalogue.test.tsx::EmojiPicker with a catalogue > fetches the grid past its first eight rows only as it nears the screen while the browser asks to save data)").
- Lightbox: pressing a thumbnail starts fetching its full image before the viewer opens (a mouse's press, or a finger's or pen's tap as it lifts without having moved, so a scroll that starts on a thumbnail fetches nothing), and the viewer fetches the image shown first (`fetchPriority="high"`), then the ones a step either side, then the rest (`fetchPriority="low"`).
