- New: `Calendar` and `DatePicker`. `Calendar` is an ARIA grid with one Tab stop (arrows, Home and End, Page Up and Page Down, Shift for years) that picks one day or, with `range`, a start and an end; `min`, `max` and `isDateDisabled` make days unavailable, and `locale` sets the names, digits and first day of the week through `Intl`. `DatePicker` is a field on the `Field` contract: a text box for typing the date with a button that opens the calendar on desktops, and the phone's own `<input type="date">` on touch screens; both submit the ISO date (`2026-10-11`) under `name`. Every button label and the invalid-date message are props with English defaults.
