- New: content that scrolls sideways shows that it scrolls. A ScrollArea (and so a Table), a CodeBlock and a Chart's visible table, while their content is wider than they are, fade it out at the side where it goes on, following the scroll and resizing. A ScrollArea's focus ring is now drawn on its root, and a CodeBlock's on an element over its code, so the fade leaves them whole; the ScrollArea's scrollbar thumb is `CanvasText` in forced colors.
