It scrolls sideways inside its own region on a phone, and `stickyHeader` keeps the header at the top inside a height you set, while it also scrolls sideways. With `rowHref` (a function, or the name of a field, which a Server Component can pass) each row is one link with one tab stop, named by its row header and at least 44px tall on a touch screen, and buttons, links and inputs in its cells keep their own taps. `striped`, `dense`, a column's `align` and `wrap`, and an `empty` message ("Nothing here yet." by default).
