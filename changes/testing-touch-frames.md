- `commands.touchDrag` and `commands.touchStep` now wait until every frame of the page is watched before they send a touch. Before, a touch into an iframe added just before it could fail with "the page did not handle the touchStart", because Playwright had not listed the new frame yet. When a touch is not handled, the error now also names each frame of the page, whether it was watched, and every call into a frame that failed and why. If the frames cannot all be watched within 5 seconds, the command throws without sending the touch.
