- Changed: control motion and feel. Hover styles on Tabs, SegmentedControl, Accordion, Collapsible, Button, Badge, Toggle, Link, FileField, Checkbox, RadioGroup, NumberField, Calendar and the Message list and attachments apply only to a fine pointer that can hover (`pointer-fine:hover:`), so a tap on a phone leaves no hover state behind; Link, the FileField drop zone, the NumberField steppers and the ColorField swatch now respond the moment the pointer goes down, and the ScrollArea thumb darkens while dragged; Tabs and SegmentedControl no longer animate when an arrow key moves the selection, and their indicator and thumb slide on `translate` only (Tabs' indicator changes size at once) in 200ms instead of a 750ms spring; the Switch thumb stretches by scaling and slides in 200ms, the Checkbox tick fades and grows in from 95% instead of drawing along its path, and the RadioGroup dot grows in from 95% instead of from nothing, in 150ms; the Slider thumb grows in 160ms; Progress fills by sliding a full-width bar (its indicator is now always full width, so an app that read the indicator's width should read its position, or the value); Chart marks draw in full at once instead of animating for up to 1.5s, and the `data-ouro-chart-animating` attribute is gone; Heading tracks tighter as text grows (none at 16px, -0.025em at 30px) instead of one fixed `tracking-tight`; the soft Button, soft Badge and pressed Toggle get a visible border under `prefers-contrast: more`; enter and exit fades on Accordion, Collapsible, Field messages, Button, Avatar, ShowMore, ScrollArea and Tabs panels use the `--ouro-ease-out` curve, and border and tint changes use `ease`.
