- New: `Avatar` and `AvatarGroup`. `name` is required: it is the accessible name and gives the initials shown until the picture loads or when it fails (`initials` and `alt` override it, and `alt=""` hides a decorative avatar). Sizes `xs` to `xl`. `AvatarGroup` stacks avatars, takes `label`, `max`, `total` and `moreLabel`, and shows a "+N" count.
