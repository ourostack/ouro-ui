- Changed: ThemePicker's looks are the options of a listbox (`role="option"` with `aria-selected`, in place of toggle buttons with `aria-pressed` in a group), so a screen reader gives each look's name, its place in the list ("2 of 7") and whether it is selected, and a choice is spoken through that state; the keys are as before. Its `controls` part `preset` is now an `option`, an app's own test that finds a look with `getByRole("button")` uses `getByRole("option")`, and keyboard focus is ringed 6px out, beyond the chosen look's ring, so a focused look never reads as the chosen one.
