- New: `Combobox`, a text input that filters `items` as people type, on the `Field` contract. It picks one item, several as chips with `multiple`, or any text with `allowCustomValue`; `onInputChange` and `loading` search on your server, and "No results" waits until something is typed. On a phone the field is a button that opens a full-height bottom sheet with the search input pinned at the top, with a clear button beside it; with `allowCustomValue` the sheet's first row keeps the typed text. `loadingLabel`, `emptyLabel`, `clearLabel`, `doneLabel`, `removeLabel` and `customValueLabel` translate its text.
