- New: `ShowMore`, which cuts a list to `initialCount` items, clamps text to `lines` lines, or grows a list a page at a time with `onShowMore`, `hasMore`, `loading` and a no-JavaScript `href`. "Show more" moves focus to the first newly shown item, a list that loads more says how many items now show (`loadedLabel`), and `showMoreLabel` and `showLessLabel` translate the button.
