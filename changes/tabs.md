- New: `Tabs`, with `items` (each `{ value, label, content }`) or the parts in `tabs-parts` (`TabsRoot`, `TabsList`, `TabsTab`, `TabsIndicator`, `TabsPanel`), `orientation` (`horizontal` or `vertical`) and `activation` (`automatic` or `manual`). A line slides to the selected tab with the theme spring, tabs are 44px tall on touch screens, a row that scrolls fades out on the side that hides more tabs, vertical tabs become a row on a phone, and the selected tab and its panel name each other in server HTML.
