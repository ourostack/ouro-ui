- New: `Sidebar`, a column of links in `sections` with `header` and `footer`, the current page marked `aria-current="page"` from `currentPath`. From 640px up it collapses to a rail of icons with its own button (`collapsed`, `defaultCollapsed`, `onCollapsedChange`, `collapseLabel`; `collapsible={false}` removes it), and each link keeps its name; below 640px it is a menu button that opens the links as a `Sheet` drawer.
- New: `SidebarLayout` and `StackedLayout`, the shells of an app with a `Sidebar` or a `Navbar`. Each renders the page's `<main>`, starts with a "Skip to content" link (`skipLabel`) and keeps its navigation in view while the page scrolls; `bleed` removes the padding around the page. `SidebarLayout` shows a top bar with the sidebar's menu button and its `header` on a phone; `StackedLayout` centers the page in a column and keeps `footer` at the bottom.
