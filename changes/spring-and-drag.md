- New: `lib/ouro/spring` and `lib/ouro/use-drag`, the gesture helpers behind `SwipeActions` and `Reorderable`, with no animation library. `spring({ from, to, velocity, onUpdate })` runs a spring on animation frames from an element's live position with its release velocity (px/ms), critically damped unless `damping: 0.8` is passed after a flick, and returns `stop()`, `retarget(to)` and `running`; with reduced motion it jumps to the target. `project`, `nearest` and `rubberband` are the math for snap points and for resistance past an end. `useDrag({ axis, onStart, onMove, onEnd })` returns pointer handlers for a one-axis drag: it waits 10px before choosing the axis, leaves the other axis to the page's scroll, captures the pointer only once the drag has begun, ignores a second finger, and reports movement from the grab point with a velocity and whether the release was a flick. Set `data-ouro-settling` on the element while a spring runs, so tests can wait for it to clear.
