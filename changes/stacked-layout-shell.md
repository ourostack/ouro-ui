- New: `StackedLayout` takes `toolbar`, a second bar right under the navbar for the page's own tools (usually a `Toolbar`), full width with the navbar's padding and its own line below, which sticks, scrolls away or hides with the navbar ([docs](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/docs/StackedLayout.md), [test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/sidebar/stacked-layout.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/sidebar/stacked-layout.test.tsx::StackedLayout > keeps the toolbar right under the navbar, full width with the navbar's padding, and in view with it)")).
- New: `StackedLayout` takes `navbarBehavior`: `"sticky"` (the default, as before), `"static"`, where the bars scroll away with a document, and `"auto-hide"`, where translucent bars slide up out of view on a scroll down and come back on a scroll up, at the top of the page or when keyboard focus moves into them, never hiding while keyboard focus or an open menu is in them, and showing and hiding at once with reduced motion ([docs](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/docs/StackedLayout.md), [test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/sidebar/stacked-layout.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/sidebar/stacked-layout.test.tsx::StackedLayout with navbarBehavior auto-hide > slides the navbar and toolbar up out of view on a scroll down, and back on a scroll up)")).
