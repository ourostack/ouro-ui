- New: ShowMore takes `open` and `onOpenChange`, so an app can set whether a list cut to its first items, text clamped to `lines` or content folded to `maxHeight` shows in full (to reveal a match from find in page, say); its button then asks `onOpenChange` and nothing changes until the app sets `open`. Without `open`, ShowMore keeps its own state as before and still calls `onOpenChange`. Text the app shows whole keeps its "Show less" button.
