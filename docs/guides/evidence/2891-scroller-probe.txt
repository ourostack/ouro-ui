# Ouro's scrollers under the compositor's LCD-text path (ourostack/ouro-ui#2891)
#
# Source: `node scripts/scroller-probe.mjs layers` and `node scripts/scroller-probe.mjs frames`, run on macOS with
# Playwright's Chromium, which the script starts with --enable-lcd-text --force-device-scale-factor=1. A see-through
# run forces the scroller's background to transparent; an opaque run is the component as this change ships it.
# A late frame is a requestAnimationFrame gap over 24 ms. Paint time is the sum of the trace's Paint events.

layers
scroll-area see-through: 5 layers, 0 holding the scrolled content (5213px)
scroll-area opaque: 9 layers, 1 holding the scrolled content (5213px)
conversation-list see-through: 8 layers, 0 holding the scrolled content (9998px)
conversation-list opaque: 10 layers, 1 holding the scrolled content (9998px)
message-list see-through: 6 layers, 0 holding the scrolled content (6297px)
message-list opaque: 8 layers, 1 holding the scrolled content (6297px)

frames
see-through: late frames 87, 63, 68 (median 68) of 388, 413, 412; paint ms 603, 581, 575 (median 581); scrolled 99649px at 4x CPU throttle
opaque: late frames 79, 19, 17 (median 19) of 394, 461, 465; paint ms 580, 530, 534 (median 534); scrolled 99649px at 4x CPU throttle

# The app's own figure, from the issue: on a CI Windows runner, a scroll-through-history case, late frames fell from 339 to 100
# and paint time from 3.6 s to 0.8 s once its message-list scroller had the opaque background of the surface behind it.
