# Changes in this release

- Added: Ouro AX: `ax-precise-copy`, `vagueFailureIn` and `vagueFailuresIn`, which find a vague failure ("couldn't", "could not", "something went wrong", "unable to", "failed to load", "an error occurred", a bare "Error" or "Failed") in a message or an answer, and `checkFailureCopyAllow`, an allow-list whose every entry needs a match and a reason ([test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/ax/precise-copy.test.mts "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/ax/precise-copy.test.mts::an allow-list needs a match and a reason in every entry)"))
- Added: Ouro AX: the X-ray's must claim `copy.precise-failure`: every refusal, error, outcome error and summary, `human.reason`, doctor finding and `help --json` text says what happened and why, not only "couldn't" or "something went wrong"; `--failure-copy-allow <file>` lets precise text through with its reason; the claim set and the X-ray are at 1.5.0 ([test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/ax/xray.test.mts "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/ax/xray.test.mts::a CLI whose failures say something went wrong fails copy.precise-failure once per text, and an allow-list with reasons lets precise text through)"))
