# Changes in this release

- Fixed: Form's error summary is inserted two frames after focus moves to the first invalid field, not before it, so a screen reader speaks the field and then the summary ([test](registry/ouro/components/ouro/form/form.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/form/form.test.tsx::Form error summary > opens the alert two frames after focus moves to the field, so the summary is announced after the field)")).
- Fixed: a named MessageList keeps the reader's place when every message gets a new key at once, [as its measured heights carry over by position](registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.test.tsx::MessageList switching conversations > holds the reader's place through a full re-key of a named conversation, with its rows not yet measured, in messages of varied heights)"); without `conversation`, such a re-key is still taken for a switch to the newest message.
- New: Cover, a thing's cover: its picture, or the first letter of its title in the theme's heading font on a wash from the muted color toward the primary, which a theme sets with `--ouro-cover-from`, `--ouro-cover-to` and `--ouro-cover-ink` ([test](registry/ouro/components/ouro/cover/cover.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/cover/cover.test.tsx::Cover > draws the title's first letter in the heading font, in the primary toward the foreground, on a wash from the muted color toward the primary, without a picture)")); at `sm`, `md`, `lg` or `fill` for a page's hero ([test](registry/ouro/components/ouro/cover/cover.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/cover/cover.test.tsx::Cover > is 40, 56 and 96px square at sm, md and lg, and as wide as its container at fill)")), falling back to the initial when the picture fails ([test](registry/ouro/components/ouro/cover/cover.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/cover/cover.test.tsx::Cover > falls back to the initial when the picture fails to load)")).
- Fixed: an error line no longer says the tool twice when the failing thing is the tool itself, as in a one-command CLI (`notes: ENOENT: ...`, not `notes: notes: ENOENT: ...`) ([test](registry/ouro/ax/problem.test.mts "verify:fileLine(registry/ouro/ax/problem.test.mts, /is not said twice|is one of a, b/)")).
- Fixed: a popup opened by a press right after a script scrolled the page, before that scroll's event came, no longer takes the page back to where it was before the scroll while a closed popup is mounted elsewhere on it ([test](registry/ouro/lib/ouro/page-scroll-hold.test.ts "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/lib/ouro/page-scroll-hold.test.ts::holdPageAtPress > holds the page from where a script scrolled it just before the press, whose scroll event comes after, with a closed popup's portal on the page)")).
- Fixed: Popover, Tooltip, HoverCard, NavigationMenu and Autocomplete popups drew their first frame about a window's height lower and then jumped into place; every Ouro popup, Menu, Select, Combobox and DatePicker included, now places itself fixed from its first frame, so it opens in place ([first-frame test](registry/ouro/components/ouro/popover/popover.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/popover/popover.test.tsx::Popover first frame > is in place from its first visible frame when its trigger opens it)")).
- New: Timer's `variant="ring"` sets the time, in light figures, and its Start, Pause or Resume inside a ring whose arc is the time left, with Cancel or Restart under it, for a screen where the timer is the focus, such as a cooking step ([test](registry/ouro/components/ouro/timer/timer.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/timer/timer.test.tsx::Timer ring > draws the time and its main button inside a ring whose arc is the time left, and Cancel under it)")).
