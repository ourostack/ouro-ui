# Changes in this release

- New: Lightbox, a grid of image thumbnails that opens a full-screen viewer on the one pressed, steps through the images with the arrow keys, buttons, a trackpad or a swipe, and announces the position once per change; with `open`, `index`, `onOpenChange` and `onIndexChange` it is the viewer alone, opened from thumbnails the app lays out.
- New: `Table` columns sort from their headers with `sortable` (or `sortValue`), uncontrolled from `defaultSort` or controlled with `sort` and `onSortChange`, and `TableHead` takes `sorted` and `onSort` for parts; the sorted header carries `aria-sort` and an arrow, and each sort is announced ([docs](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/docs/Table.md), [test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/table/table.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/table/table.test.tsx::Table sorting > sorts by a sortable column from its header, and reverses on the next press, with aria-sort and an arrow)")).
