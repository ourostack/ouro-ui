# Changes in this release

- Changed: on a phone, [`<Combobox multiple>`](registry/ouro/components/ouro/combobox/combobox.touch.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/combobox/combobox.touch.test.tsx::Combobox multiple on a phone > shows each pick as a chip beside the field's button, wrapping onto more lines, with no text cut)") shows each pick as a chip with a Remove button, and the field grows as the chips wrap. Before, it showed the picks as one line of text cut off at the field's end. A tap on the field outside a Remove button still opens the sheet.
- Fixed: [ConversationList's open row keeps its selected fill alone on hover](registry/ouro/components/ouro/conversation-list/conversation-list.hover.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/conversation-list/conversation-list.hover.test.tsx::ConversationList hover > draws a hovered row as a raised card with 6px corners, and a hovered selected row in the selected tokens)"), dropping the plain rows' hover ring that made it look raised off the list, as if it were not the open one (#3477).
