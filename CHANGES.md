# Changes in this release

- New: Ouro check's [`narrow-text` rule](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/test/check-in-page.test.ts "verify:testExists(test/check-in-page.test.ts::findSizeIssues: narrow-text > flags prose a max-width holds under three quarters of a column at least 900px wide, naming the cap and the rule that set it)") finds prose that a max-width holds under three quarters of a column at least 900px wide, and names the rule that set the cap. Let the text use its column, or [declare the measure, at 40ch or wider](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/test/check-in-page.test.ts "verify:testExists(test/check-in-page.test.ts::findSizeIssues: narrow-text > leaves a measure the page declares at 40ch or wider, by data-ouro-measure or a --measure-* token, and flags one declared narrower)"), with a `--measure-*` token, `data-ouro-measure` or `ouro-check.json`; a declared measure leaves the text alone.
- Added: [Sidebar's StatusBadges story passes a Badge as an item's badge](registry/ouro/components/ouro/sidebar/sidebar.stories.tsx "verify:fileLine(registry/ouro/components/ouro/sidebar/sidebar.stories.tsx, /badge: <Badge tone=\"warning\">Idle<\/Badge>/)"), as an app's agent list does, so the story scans draw a badge given as an element.
