# Changes in this release

- Fixed: the test kit's text contrast check measures a paragraph taller than the screen part by part, scrolling each part onto the screen, so faint text whose background darkens below the fold fails instead of passing ([test](registry/ouro/lib/ouro/testing/axe.test.ts "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/lib/ouro/testing/axe.test.ts::audit > fails a tall paragraph whose gradient darkens below the fold, and names the part below the fold that fails)")).
- Fixed: Moment's art [no longer fades in from opacity 0 on its first frame when it is ready at mount](registry/ouro/components/ouro/moment/moment.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/moment/moment.test.tsx::Moment arrival > draws a moment that is ready at mount at full opacity in its first frame, with no arrival)"): the arrival runs only for content that comes in after mount, or on every mount with the new `appear` prop, and [a guard holds Moment's stories to it](test/first-frame.test.tsx "verify:testExists(test/first-frame.test.tsx::first frame guard > finds content that fades in after its first frame)").
- Fixed: an icon-only Button is at least as wide as it is tall, not a fixed width, so [a single image or logo child keeps its own width](registry/ouro/components/ouro/button/button.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/button/button.test.tsx::icon-only size > keeps an image child at its own width, and the button is never narrower than it is tall)"); an icon still sits in a square, and an app's `className` width or padding still wins.
- New: Moment's art [arrives once](registry/ouro/components/ouro/moment/moment.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/moment/moment.test.tsx::Moment arrival > arrives once when the moment first shows with appear, from the declared keyframes over the sheet duration and easing)") when the moment first shows with `appear`, [rising 10px and growing from 96%](registry/ouro/components/ouro/moment/moment.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/moment/moment.test.tsx::Moment arrival > arrives once when the moment first shows with appear, from the declared keyframes over the sheet duration and easing)") over `--ouro-duration-sheet`, [again when its kind changes](registry/ouro/components/ouro/moment/moment.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/moment/moment.test.tsx::Moment arrival > arrives again when its kind changes, and a moment with a key arrives once a session even when it remounts)"), and [not at all under reduced motion](registry/ouro/components/ouro/moment/moment.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/moment/moment.test.tsx::Moment arrival > has no arrival under reduced motion, or under OuroProvider's reduced motion)"). Apps that styled the art's entrance by hand can drop that CSS.
