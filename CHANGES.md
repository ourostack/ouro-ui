# Changes in this release

- New: `FamilyBar`, the slim strip above a site's own header that links every site of a family in one order, [the site being shown marked with `aria-current="true"`, heavier text and a bar under it](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/family-bar/family-bar.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/family-bar/family-bar.test.tsx::FamilyBar > renders a labelled nav of the family's links in order, the current site marked with aria-current, heavier text and a bar under it)"); [the whole bar is in the server HTML](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/family-bar/family-bar.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/family-bar/family-bar.test.tsx::FamilyBar > renders the whole bar in the server HTML)"), and on a narrow screen [its row scrolls inside itself, never the page](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/family-bar/family-bar.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/family-bar/family-bar.test.tsx::FamilyBar > scrolls the row inside itself on a narrow screen, never the page, and fades the side that hides more)").
- New: `FamilyFooter`, [the same links in the same order at the foot of the page, with the site's own extras after them](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/family-bar/family-footer.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/family-bar/family-footer.test.tsx::FamilyFooter > renders the family's links in order in a labelled nav, the current site marked as in the bar, and the site's extras after them)"); `add family-footer` installs `family-bar` with it.
- Fixed: MediaTile's `onVisibilityChange` [goes by the newest of the entries the browser hands over at once](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/media-tile/media-tile.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/media-tile/media-tile.test.tsx::MediaTile visibility > goes by the newest of the entries the browser hands over at once, so a tile that moved off screen is not left seen)"), so a tile that moved off screen while the page was busy is no longer left reported as seen.
- New: Ouro check's `image-fade` rule [finds a picture that fades into the page](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/test/check-in-page.test.ts "verify:testExists(test/check-in-page.test.ts::findImageFades > finds a picture faded by a gradient mask on itself or a box around it, by either mask property)"): an `<img>`, an SVG `<image>` or a `url()` background under a gradient mask, or under a gradient to transparent from an overlay or a `::before` or `::after`; [a picture or an overlay opts out with `data-image-fade-ok="<reason>"`](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/test/check-in-page.test.ts "verify:testExists(test/check-in-page.test.ts::findImageFades > passes a picture with a hard edge, on a solid band, beside or under a gradient, hidden, or allowed with a reason)").
- New: `ouro-check` installs `lib/ouro/check/fades.ts`, which [finds every gradient used as a mask in an app's source](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/scripts/check/fades.test.mts "verify:testExists(scripts/check/fades.test.mts::the fade check > finds a gradient mask in Tailwind: an arbitrary property or value, and every gradient mask utility, under any variant)") (stylesheets, style attributes, Tailwind classes and style objects) and [exits 3 with the file, the line and the fix for each](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/scripts/check/fades.test.mts "verify:testExists(scripts/check/fades.test.mts::the fade check's command > prints each finding with its place, its declaration and the fix, and exits 3)"); a mask that fades text or controls stays when [its line or the line before says why, `fade-ok: <reason>`](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/scripts/check/fades.test.mts "verify:testExists(scripts/check/fades.test.mts::the fade check > passes a gradient mask that says why on its line or the line before, and only with a reason)").
- New: `Porthole`, the status spot: one line about how current the app's data is, [the most urgent item first](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/porthole/porthole.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/porthole/porthole.test.tsx::Porthole > shows the most urgent item, and the rest line when there is none)"), opening on hover or press into the facts behind it; it owns its clock, so [a stopped heartbeat reads "Status unknown" at its timeout](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/porthole/porthole.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/porthole/porthole.test.tsx::Porthole timing > says Status unknown once at the heartbeat's timeout when the beats stop, and recovers on the next beat)") and [beats never render it](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/porthole/porthole.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/porthole/porthole.test.tsx::Porthole timing > renders nothing for 60 beats over 60 s at rest, then once when fresh runs out)"); `portholeAx` declares its agent side, a `status` read and person-only actions ([docs](https://ui.ouro.bot/docs/porthole/)).
