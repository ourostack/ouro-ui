# Changes in this release

- Changed: ScrollArea's viewport and MessageList's scroller now paint `--ouro-scroller-surface` (the page's `--background`, or the fill of the Card, Popover, Menu, Select, Combobox, Navigation menu, Dialog or Sheet around them), so Chromium on Windows can composite them; a scroller on another fill sets the token, and on glass sets it to `transparent`.
- New: [Item takes a `chevron` prop on a linked row](registry/ouro/components/ouro/item/item.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/item/item.test.tsx::Item with href > draws chevron as decorative, after actions on the trailing edge)"), drawing a decorative trailing chevron after `actions` so a row that navigates reads as tappable, as a settings list shows; opt-in and off by default, mirrored under `dir="rtl"`.
- Fixed: [MessageList puts back a scroll of a few pixels it did not write, just after it held the reader's place](registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.prepend.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.prepend.test.tsx::MessageList prepending older history > puts back a scroll of a few pixels at rest that neither the reader nor the list made, just after the list held the reader's place)"), so Chromium's own pixel or two in the next frame no longer moves what the reader is reading; [a scroll code asks for with scrollTo or scrollBy, such as a find's smooth scroll to its match, goes all the way](registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.prepend.test.tsx "verify:fileLine(registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.prepend.test.tsx, /lets a smooth \$\{name\} code asks for just after the list held the reader's place go all the way/)"), [as does one after a press, a key or a touch](registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.prepend.test.tsx "verify:fileLine(registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.prepend.test.tsx, /keeps a scroll of a few pixels that comes after \$\{name\}/)"), [or frames later, or after a larger move](registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.prepend.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.prepend.test.tsx::MessageList prepending older history > keeps a scroll of a few pixels frames after the list's own write, or after a larger move)").
- SettingsPage's Save button shows it is busy and turns off while `onSave` runs, so a slow save can't be sent twice and a person sees it working.
