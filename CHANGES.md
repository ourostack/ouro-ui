# Changes in this release

- Fixed: a rich Composer inside an Autocomplete now exposes its editor as a combobox that says whether the suggestion list is open, so a screen reader announces the list.
- New: `catalogue` works in `format="rich"`: the box draws emoji tokens as the catalogue's pictures, and the Markdown value keeps the `:shortcode:` tokens.
- Fixed: [MessageList puts back a scroll of a few pixels it did not write, just after it held the reader's place](registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.prepend.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.prepend.test.tsx::MessageList prepending older history > puts back a scroll of a few pixels at rest that neither the reader nor the list made, just after the list held the reader's place)"), so Chromium's own pixel or two in the next frame no longer moves what the reader is reading; [one long after the list's write, or after a larger move, is kept](registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.prepend.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.prepend.test.tsx::MessageList prepending older history > keeps a scroll of a few pixels long after the list's own write, or after a larger move)"), and [so is one after a press, a key or a touch](registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.prepend.test.tsx "verify:fileLine(registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.prepend.test.tsx, /keeps a scroll of a few pixels that comes after \$\{name\}/)").
- New: the Ouro AX X-ray checks a harness's in-turn tools (`--tools "<command>"`, JSON-RPC over stdio): `tools.*` claims in [claim set 1.8.0](registry/ouro/ax/ax-claims.ts "verify:fileLine(registry/ouro/ax/ax-claims.ts, /CLAIM_SET_VERSION = .1\.8\.0./)") for function definitions, a declared effect (must), a `when` line, a consequential tool's confirm question (must), bad calls refused naming the argument, typed outcomes whose codes the card lists, an unknown tool, and the inventory's `tools` list (must).
