# Changes in this release

- Fixed: the census kit's `censusTests` registers its tests under Playwright, whose hooks need an object pattern for their fixtures, and the guide's example declares the census in the app's own spec so a run of that file lists it.
- Fixed: Ouro check's design rules judge only what is drawn. A box counts as the part of it its clipping boxes leave, and a box scrolled out of its scroller, such as a card past the edge of a map, is no longer [the box a heading sits cramped under](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/test/check-design-in-page.test.ts "verify:testExists(test/check-design-in-page.test.ts::findDesignIssues: cramped-heading > measures a box by the part of it that is drawn: cut by a clipping ancestor, or nothing at all once scrolled out)"); absolutely positioned and fixed boxes [escape the clips they're drawn outside](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/test/check-design-in-page.test.ts "verify:testExists(test/check-design-in-page.test.ts::findDesignIssues: cramped-heading > lets an absolutely positioned box escape a clip below its positioned ancestor, and a fixed one escape every clip)").
- Fixed: Ouro check [reads a page's first content before any rule scrolls it](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/scripts/check/check.test.mts "verify:testExists(scripts/check/check.test.mts::Ouro check > measures layout shift, first content and each press against their budgets, presses at one size and theme, and records each number)"), so content the page draws after the corner rule's scroll no longer reads as slow first content.
- Fixed: Ouro check's `baked-corner` [passes a corner that shows the page as drawn at its tip, or the frame's own outline or border](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/test/check-design-in-page.test.ts "verify:testExists(test/check-design-in-page.test.ts::readCorners > passes a corner that shows the page as drawn at its tip, or a colour the frame draws itself, such as its outline or border)"), as a chosen card's offset outline or a picture's own hairline border draws.
- Fixed: Ouro check's jank rule judges a step only on its full set of runs, so one slow frame on a busy machine [makes no finding](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/scripts/check/check.test.mts "verify:testExists(scripts/check/check.test.mts::Ouro check's design rules > judges jank only on a step's full runs: a covered target that returns is waited for, and one that never returns skips the step)") when the step's target can't be reached again.
- Fixed: the claims checker reads the repository from package.json's `repository` field, then from the `origin` remote, and names the missing field when it finds neither, rather than assuming Ouro UI's own repository.
- Fixed: Ouro Feel's probe [counts a blank frame by the rows' boxes, so a list that turns off pointer events while it scrolls is not blank](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/scripts/feel.test.mjs "verify:testExists(scripts/feel.test.mjs::Ouro Feel's probe counts a blank frame by the rows' boxes, so a list that turns off pointer events while it scrolls is not blank, and a gap is)"), and reads the rows once a frame.
- New: Ouro check's `stretched-image` rule [finds a picture or a video drawn out of its own shape](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/test/check-in-page.test.ts "verify:testExists(test/check-in-page.test.ts::findSizeIssues: stretched-image > finds a picture drawn out of its own shape, taller or wider, small ones too, by how much)"): a width and a height that don't keep its ratio by more than 5% and 2px, with `object-fit: fill`. Fit it with `object-fit: cover`, or give it one size and let the other follow. `data-size-ignore` covers it, as it covers `blurry-image`.
- New: Ouro check's `disabled-no-reason` rule [finds a disabled control with no reason tied to it](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/test/check-interaction-in-page.test.ts "verify:testExists(test/check-interaction-in-page.test.ts::findInteractionIssues: disabled-no-reason > finds a disabled control the page gives no reason for, however it is turned off, named by its words)"): no `title`, `aria-description` or `aria-describedby` text, and not a control that moves one way at its end or one beside an empty field it waits for.
- Fixed: on Ouro's site, Nautilus's [Launch](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/site/src/voyage/chapters/chapter-one.tsx "verify:fileLine(site/src/voyage/chapters/chapter-one.tsx, /aria-describedby=\{hint\}/)") and [Play the organ](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/site/src/voyage/chapters/chapter-three.tsx "verify:fileLine(site/src/voyage/chapters/chapter-three.tsx, /aria-describedby=\{hint\}/)") are described by the hint beside them, so a screen reader says why the button is off.
- New: Ouro check's layout rules: `off-screen` [finds text or a control the screen cuts off where nothing scrolls to it](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/test/check-layout-in-page.test.ts "verify:testExists(test/check-layout-in-page.test.ts::findLayoutIssues: off-screen > finds a control or text the screen cuts off at either edge, once for a nest, named by its words)"), `duplicate-control` [finds the same control twice in view](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/test/check-layout-in-page.test.ts "verify:testExists(test/check-layout-in-page.test.ts::findLayoutIssues: duplicate-control > finds two controls in view with the same role and name, once a pair, marking both)"), and `stray-scrollbar` [finds a region that scrolls by a few pixels](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/test/check-layout-in-page.test.ts "verify:testExists(test/check-layout-in-page.test.ts::findLayoutIssues: stray-scrollbar > finds a region that scrolls by a few pixels, either way, and a page a few pixels taller than the screen)").
- Changed: a `MessageList`'s action bar [shows on the pointer's first message adding no event listener](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.actions.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.actions.test.tsx::MessageList actions > shows its first bar adding no event listener, so WebKit restyles nothing as the pointer first reaches a message)"): its portal is there from the layer's first render, so React's listeners (a wheel listener among them, which restyles the whole page in WebKit, 151 ms on a long conversation) go on as the list arrives rather than on the first hover.
- Fixed: a `MessageList` mounted after another has loaded the action bar's code [starts with the bar layer](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.actions.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.actions.test.tsx::MessageList actions > has its bar layer in its first commit once an earlier list has loaded the layer's code)"), rather than getting it a commit after its first.
- Fixed: NavigationRail's shipped font-load fit test brings its own font, so it passes in an installed app whatever fonts the machine has.
- Fixed: NumberField's steppers pass Base UI a render function whose name a minifier can't turn into a capitalised one, so a minified development build stops logging Base UI's render-prop warning.
- New: the `ouro-check` agent plugin ([`plugins/ouro-check`](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/tree/main/plugins/ouro-check)) teaches an agent to run Ouro check and fix what its JSON findings report. Install it with `agency plugin install github:ourostack/ouro-ui:plugins/ouro-check@main --cache-policy auto --cache-ttl 0`. It moved here from `ourostack/ouroboros-skills`.
- Fixed: Ouro check's `overlap` [no longer reports page text under an opaque overlay](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/test/check-in-page.test.ts "verify:testExists(test/check-in-page.test.ts::findOverlaps > passes page text under an opaque overlay: a dialog, a menu, a popover or a floating panel, on a fill of its own or a box in it)") (an open dialog, menu, popover or floating panel), and `clipped-text` [no longer reports an ellipsis behind an open modal](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/test/check-in-page.test.ts "verify:testExists(test/check-in-page.test.ts::findSizeIssues: clipped-text > passes an ellipsis behind an open modal, which hides the page on purpose, but not one the modal itself hides)"), which hides the page on purpose.
- Fixed: Ouro check's `axe` [leaves out Base UI's focus guards](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/test/check-in-page.test.ts "verify:testExists(test/check-in-page.test.ts::runAxe > leaves out Base UI's focus guards, as Ouro's axe kit does, but not another focusable element under aria-hidden)"), as the axe kit's `axeExclude` does, so an open menu's guards no longer read as `aria-hidden-focus`.
- Fixed: Porthole [draws its width at once when its column shows again](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/porthole/porthole.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/porthole/porthole.test.tsx::Porthole > draws its width at once when its column shows again, never easing from the width it had while hidden)"). It used to measure its pill while hidden, then ease from that narrow width to its line's once shown, so a sidebar shown again drew a dot that grew into the line after the first frame. A change of line while the pill is drawn still eases.
- Changed: the `press` utility [changes colors at once](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/theme/theme.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/theme/theme.test.tsx::press utility > draws a selected row's background on the frame it is selected, and takes it off the row it left on that frame)"): a selected, current or pressed control has its background, border and text colour on the frame it becomes so, and the control it took over from loses them on that frame. `press` used to ease them, so a clicked row was drawn transparent for its first frames. Hover changes at once too, and its scale still eases. `NavigationRail`'s current item changes colour at once; its bar still grows. `Checkbox` keeps its own fill-then-tick motion. `ConversationList`'s rows drop their own `transition-none` classes: `press` now gives them the same instant colours.
- Changed: a `MessageList`'s action bar, arriving after its list, [shows on a row already focused from the keyboard or under a resting pointer in the commit it arrives in](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/lib/ouro/row-hover.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/lib/ouro/row-hover.test.tsx::useRowHover > shows on a row focused from the keyboard, or the pointer rests on, in the commit a layer arriving after its list mounts in)"): `useRowHover` listens in the layout phase when the list's element is already there, so that row's bar no longer waits for a later task.
- New: Ouro check's `console-error` and `failed-request` rules [find an uncaught error, a `console.error` or `console.warn`, and a request to the page's own origin that answers 400 or more or not at all](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/scripts/check/check.test.mts "verify:testExists(scripts/check/check.test.mts::Ouro check > finds each rule on its fail page and nothing on any pass page, with a report, a contact sheet and close-ups)"), one finding for one failure: the page's own document stays `http-error`'s and a picture `broken-image`'s.
- Fixed: a vertical ScrollArea keeps its content to the viewport's width, so a child that can't wrap overflows on its own and the text beside it still wraps.
- Fixed: `SyncStatus` [waits 1.5 seconds before it announces going offline or an error](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/sync-status/sync-status.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/sync-status/sync-status.test.tsx::SyncStatus > leaves time for a control's state announcement before speaking %s, and speaks its recovery without that wait)"), so the switch or button that caused it can announce its own state first; VoiceOver used to speak only the switch ([#857](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/issues/857)). `announce()` now [returns a function that cancels its pending message](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/lib/ouro/announce.test.ts "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/lib/ouro/announce.test.ts::announce > can cancel a pending message without cancelling a later caller's message)").
- New: Ouro check's `leaked-value` rule [finds words the program meant for itself on screen](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/test/check-text-in-page.test.ts "verify:testExists(test/check-text-in-page.test.ts::findTextIssues: leaked-value > finds each kind of value the program meant for itself, quoting what it shows)"): `[object Object]`, an unfilled template, a GUID or a long hex id, a translation key, `lorem ipsum`, a developer's note (`TODO:`, `FIXME`), `console.log`, `NaN`, and `undefined` or `null` [where a value goes, not as a word in a sentence](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/test/check-text-in-page.test.ts "verify:testExists(test/check-text-in-page.test.ts::findTextIssues: leaked-value > finds undefined and null where a value goes: alone, after a label, twice, after a greeting, beside a number)"), in drawn text, a `title` or an `aria-label`.
- New: Ouro check's `odd-timestamp` rule [finds a time no person should read](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/test/check-text-in-page.test.ts "verify:testExists(test/check-text-in-page.test.ts::findTextIssues: odd-timestamp > finds each kind of time no person should read, quoting what it shows)"): `Invalid Date`, the Unix epoch, the year 0000, 0001 or 9999, a raw epoch number, a machine timestamp, `Date`'s own string or a negative age, judged without the clock.
- New: Ouro check's `focus-ring` rule [finds a control whose keyboard focus changes nothing a person can see](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/test/check-theme-in-page.test.ts "verify:testExists(test/check-theme-in-page.test.ts::findThemeIssues: focus-ring > finds a control whose keyboard focus changes nothing a person can see, a transparent or 0-wide ring too)"): no outline, shadow, border, fill, colour or underline on it, its frame, what is in it or a pseudo-element, a transparent or 0-wide ring counting as none.
- New: Ouro check's `dark-miss` rule [finds a light neutral surface left on a dark page](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/test/check-theme-in-page.test.ts "verify:testExists(test/check-theme-in-page.test.ts::findThemeIssues: dark-miss > finds a light neutral surface on a dark page, once for a nest, by its fill and the page's)"), leaving accents, controls, inversions and surfaces on a picture alone.
- New: Ouro check's `blurry-image` [cannot be waived](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/test/check-in-page.test.ts "verify:testExists(test/check-in-page.test.ts::findSizeIssues: blurry-image > cannot be waived: reports an attempt to allow it, and still finds the picture)"): `data-ouro-allow="blurry-image"` is a finding of its own and still reports the picture, `data-size-ignore`, `"*"` and `all` no longer cover it, and [a waiver of it in `ouro-check.json` exits 2](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/scripts/check/check.test.mts "verify:testExists(scripts/check/check.test.mts::Ouro check's command > exits 2 when Playwright, axe-core, the config, the earlier report, a shard's report or the pages are missing, and 1 when it cannot run)").
- New: `blurry-image` [holds the drawn width times the density to the file's width exactly](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/test/check-in-page.test.ts "verify:testExists(test/check-in-page.test.ts::findSizeIssues: blurry-image > holds the drawn width times the density to the file's width exactly, with half a pixel for rounding)") rather than with 10% slack, and [checks raster CSS backgrounds](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/test/check-in-page.test.ts "verify:testExists(test/check-in-page.test.ts::findSizeIssues: blurry-image, for a CSS background > finds a raster background drawn larger than its file at the density, by its background-size)").
- New: Ouro check's `--images-report <file.json>` [writes each blurry picture with the file it drew and the width it needs](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/scripts/check/check.test.mts "verify:testExists(scripts/check/check.test.mts::Ouro check > never waives a blurry picture, and lists each one, with the file it needs, in the images report)"), for a tool that replaces them; `merge` takes it too.
- New: `ouro-images` [refuses a picture too small to draw sharp](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/scripts/images/images.test.mts "verify:testExists(scripts/images/images.test.mts::Ouro images' refusal of pictures too small to draw sharp > refuses a picture narrower than twice its widest drawn width, or under the long-edge floor, and passes one at the line)"): narrower than twice `--max-width`, shorter on its long edge than `--min-long-edge`, or under 2048 on its long edge in a `hero`, `heroes`, `scene` or `scenes` folder.
