# Changes in this release

- Changed: [the Cookbook theme's controls are Apple's continuous capsule](site/scripts/theme-corner-shape.test.mjs "verify:testExists(site/scripts/theme-corner-shape.test.mjs::the Cookbook theme makes its controls continuous capsules: a full control radius in superellipse(1.05), never round, and checks on the theme's corners)"): `--ouro-control-corner-shape: superellipse(1.05)` beside its 9999px radius, so a button's ends read as a pill, as in Spoonjoy's Apple app, not as a rounded box. [Each control's radius is half its height in that shape](registry/ouro/theme/control-shape.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/theme/control-shape.test.tsx::control shape tokens > make every control a continuous capsule at 9999px and superellipse(1.05), its radius half its height)"). Copy the Cookbook theme again to get it.
- Fixed: [where the engine has no corner-shape (Safari), a control in superellipse(1.05) is clipped to that same curve](registry/ouro/theme/control-shape.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/theme/control-shape.test.tsx::control shape tokens > clip a continuous capsule's surface to its superellipse where the engine has no corner-shape, with or without shape())"), with a `shape()` path or a polygon where `shape()` is not drawn.
- Changed: [the Cookbook theme sets `--ouro-control-radius: 9999px`](site/scripts/theme-corner-shape.test.mjs "verify:testExists(site/scripts/theme-corner-shape.test.mjs::the Cookbook theme makes its controls continuous capsules: a full control radius in superellipse(1.05), never round, and checks on the theme's corners)"), so its buttons, fields, selects and toggles are squircle capsules, as in Spoonjoy's Apple app. Its checks keep the theme's own corners. Copy the Cookbook theme again to get it.
- Changed: the Cookbook theme no longer sets `--ouro-control-radius`, `--ouro-control-corner-shape`, `--ouro-check-radius` or `--ouro-check-corner-shape`, so its controls and checks keep the theme's own squircle corners instead of round capsules and circles; copy the Cookbook theme again to get it ([test](site/scripts/theme-corner-shape.test.mjs "verify:testExists(site/scripts/theme-corner-shape.test.mjs::the Cookbook theme makes its controls continuous capsules: a full control radius in superellipse(1.05), never round, and checks on the theme's corners)")).
