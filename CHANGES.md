# Changes in this release

- New: `Alert`, an inline banner with a required `title`, `description`, `tone`, `action`, `icon` and `onDismiss` (with `dismissLabel`). Destructive and warning alerts are `role="alert"`; the others `role="status"`.
- New: `Avatar` and `AvatarGroup`. `name` is required: it is the accessible name and gives the initials shown until the picture loads or when it fails (`initials` and `alt` override it, and `alt=""` hides a decorative avatar). Avatar is always a circle, in sizes `xs` to `3xl` (24 to 112px), and the initials grow with it. `AvatarGroup` stacks avatars, takes `label`, `max`, `total` and `moreLabel`, and shows a "+N" count.
- New: `Badge`, with `tone` and `variant` (`solid`, `soft`, `outline`; default `tone="secondary" variant="solid"`). With `href` it is a link through your router, with `onClick` a button (pass `aria-pressed` for a filter chip), each with a 44px touch target on coarse pointers.
- New: `Kbd`, a keyboard shortcut from `keys={["mod", "k"]}`: ⌘K on Apple platforms and Ctrl+K elsewhere, with modifiers in the platform's order (⌃⌥⇧⌘ on Apple) and spoken names for symbols. `cmd` is Win on Windows and Ctrl on Linux and ChromeOS. Server HTML shows the key names, and Apple devices switch to the glyphs after hydration.
- New: `Separator`, on Base UI, horizontal or vertical, and `decorative` to hide it from screen readers.
- New: `TextField` and `TextArea`, the first controls on the `Field` contract. `TextField` takes `type` (`text`, `email`, `password`, `tel`, `url`, `search`, `date`, `time`), which sets `inputMode` and `autoComplete`; a show/hide button for passwords; `startAdornment` and `endAdornment` in place of shadcn's InputGroup; and `clearable`, with `showPasswordLabel` and `clearLabel` to translate the button labels. `TextArea` grows with its text from `rows` to `maxRows` with `autoGrow`.
- Text controls (`FieldControl`, `TextField`, `TextArea`) step down to the 36px desktop height and 14px text from the `sm` breakpoint only with a fine pointer, so a landscape phone or a touch tablet keeps 44px and 16px text, and iOS does not zoom on focus.
