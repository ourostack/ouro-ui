# Changes in this release

- Checkbox's tick draws along its path once the box has filled (`stroke-dashoffset` over the new `--ouro-duration-draw`, 240ms, after the 150ms fill) and is taken back along it when unchecked, in place of the 95% scale and fade. With reduced motion it fades in whole, and nothing draws or scales. Add `ouro-base` again for `--ouro-duration-draw` (UPGRADING.md); until then the tick appears at once. Screenshots of a checked box mid-change move.
- New: `Checklist` (`@group Lists`), a list of things to tick off, such as a recipe's ingredients or a shopping list. Pass `label`, `items` (`{ id, label, description?, value?, checked, disabled?, action? }`) and `onCheckedChange(id, checked)`. The whole row but its `action` toggles it; checking fills the box, draws the tick, then draws a strike through the label and softens the text; rows enter and leave as `items` changes (a removed row stays until its exit ends, and an id added back while it leaves returns in place); `onClearChecked` shows "Clear checked" while something is checked. Parts `ChecklistRoot` and `ChecklistItem` build rows by hand.
- Every element a person can focus or operate that an Ouro component renders, in portals too, now carries `data-ouro-control` (the registry item that rendered it, such as `select`) and `data-ouro-part` (which of its parts it is, such as `trigger` or `option`), so tests, audits and overlays can find each control by identity. An Ouro control another component renders as its own part takes that component's name (ConfirmDialog's Cancel button is `confirm-dialog`/`cancel`); a control the app passes in keeps its own (a Dialog trigger Button stays `button`/`root`).
- Combobox marks the visually hidden Dismiss buttons Base UI adds for screen readers while its list is open (`combobox`/`dismiss` beside the input, `combobox`/`popup-dismiss` beside the list), and a ResizablePanel, which takes focus when its content overflows, is `resizable`/`panel`, so a census that leaves out only Base UI's focus guards finds no unmarked Ouro control.
- New: `Inspector` (`@group App setup`), X-ray for a page built with Ouro HX. While `open`, it outlines every Ouro component with a tag naming its registry item (a link to its docs: Ouro's public docs, or `docsHref(item)`), follows them through scrolling, resizing, overlays and page changes (measured at most once a frame), and shows only what a person can see and reach: an outline is cut to the part of its component that a scrolling or clipping box lets show (hidden, tag and all, when none does), a page shut off behind an open modal dialog loses its outlines and the bar steps aside (a popover leaves both, with a combobox list open in it too), a tag hides while something else lies over it or its component's corner (an open menu, select list or combobox list, or a phone's menu sheet; not a linked card's own link stretched over its contents), so tags never land on another overlay's items, and a part's root inside its own component (each Radio of a RadioGroup) counts once with it. It shows a bar whose list (a `Sheet`) names every component in document order with Docs and, from `sourceHref(item, element)`, Source links for keyboard and screen reader users. Outlines never take a pointer, so the page stays usable; Escape or Close calls `onOpenChange(false)`; an Escape that closes another overlay first (a dialog, a combobox list, a tooltip) or that the page handled leaves X-ray open. Its own controls carry `data-ouro-control="inspector"`, so an app's census of its own controls can leave them out.
- Checkbox's tick carries `data-ouro-part="tick"` (beside the box's `box`), so a test or audit finds each animated part by identity.
- The test kit (`ouro-testing`) adds `lib/ouro/testing/motion.ts`: `expectMotion` proves exactly which CSS properties a change into a state runs on one part (the part or an element around it inside its own component must carry the state's attribute; no missing and no extra property, with durations in milliseconds when given) and waits for them to end, failing on a transition cancelled early with nothing in its place, `expectNoMotion` proves a part does not move, and `parseDuration` reads `"240ms"`, `"0.24s"` or a duration token; Checkbox's tests use them (see "Proving motion" in docs/guides/testing.md).
- An outline Button in the primary tone (`variant="outline"`, the default tone) draws its label in the foreground color, as shadcn's outline button does, keeping its primary-tinted border; `--primary` is a fill color in shadcn's themes, and a primary that only just reaches 4.5:1 on the page fell below it on a tinted surface such as a striped table row. Outline buttons look darker under the Ouro theme, and screenshots with one move.
- Shadows follow your theme: popovers, menus, selects, comboboxes, navigation menus, toasts and the chart tooltip read `--shadow-lg`, dialogs `--shadow-xl`, the slider and switch thumbs and the segmented control's indicator `--shadow-sm`, Card's outline variant `--shadow-xs` and MessageList's jump button `--shadow-md`, at runtime, so a shadcn theme that sets them (a tweakcn export, a dark theme with deeper shadows) restyles every surface. Popovers, menus, navigation menus and dialogs keep their hairline ring, now Tailwind's `ring-1` in `--border` at 60%. Without such a theme the shadows are Tailwind's `shadow-*` scale, a little different from the fixed black shadows they replace.
- Sheets, and a Menu shown as a bottom sheet on a phone, read their shadow from `--ouro-shadow-sheet-bottom`, `-top`, `-left` or `-right`, which `ouro-base` now defines with the values they had. Add `ouro-base` again to get them (UPGRADING.md); until then a sheet draws no shadow.
- Heading and the Dialog, Sheet and Popover titles use your theme's `--font-heading` (the font around them when it has none) Kbd keeps the surrounding font, as shadcn's does.
- SegmentedControl's unselected segments and Sidebar's row badges mix their muted text 20% toward the foreground, so they keep 4.5:1 on the muted surface under shadcn's stock themes (plain `--muted-foreground` there is 4.34:1).
- The test kit (`ouro-testing`) adds `lib/ouro/testing/announcements.ts`: `watchAnnouncements()` records the text each live region announces (`role="status"`, `role="alert"`, `role="log"` or `aria-live`, portals included, nothing under `aria-hidden`), once per region for each batch of DOM changes: text added to or changed in a region already on the page, and the text an assertive region (`role="alert"`, `aria-live="assertive"`) is added with, but not a new polite region's first text, nor text under `aria-live="off"` or beside a region in an added subtree. It has `changes`, `matching(text)`, `count(text)` and `stop()`, so a test proves a message is heard once per change and in which region; Toast's tests use it (see "Proving announcements" in docs/guides/testing.md).
