# Changes in this release

- Changed: [the Cookbook theme sets `--ouro-control-radius: 9999px` with no corner-shape token](site/scripts/theme-corner-shape.test.mjs "verify:testExists(site/scripts/theme-corner-shape.test.mjs::the Cookbook theme makes its controls squircle capsules: a full control radius, no corner-shape token, and checks on the theme's corners)"), so its buttons, fields, selects and toggles are squircle capsules, as in Spoonjoy's Apple app. Its checks keep the theme's own corners. Copy the Cookbook theme again to get it.
- Changed: the Cookbook theme no longer sets `--ouro-control-radius`, `--ouro-control-corner-shape`, `--ouro-check-radius` or `--ouro-check-corner-shape`, so its controls and checks keep the theme's own squircle corners instead of round capsules and circles; copy the Cookbook theme again to get it ([test](site/scripts/theme-corner-shape.test.mjs "verify:testExists(site/scripts/theme-corner-shape.test.mjs::the Cookbook theme makes its controls squircle capsules: a full control radius, no corner-shape token, and checks on the theme's corners)")).
