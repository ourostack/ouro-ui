# Changes in this release

- New: `Celebrate`, a moment of celebration: confetti bursts from a badge with the message and screen readers hear it once; with reduced motion only the badge fades in.
- New: `ChoiceCard`, choices shown as cards with a picture, a label and a description, on a native radio group under a legend: arrow keys move between them and a plain form submits the chosen value.
- New: `lib-claims`, with `claim(text, { source, verify })`, which marks a sentence of your copy with the primary source it comes from and a check that it still holds, and [the checker beside it, `lib/ouro/claims-check.mjs`, which finds the factual sentences in your Markdown and copy that have no source and runs the checks of those that do](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/scripts/claims.test.mjs "verify:testExists(scripts/claims.test.mjs::the claims checker ships in lib-claims and runs in an app)"): `node src/lib/ouro/claims-check.mjs check`, with your lib folder in place of `src/lib`. Add it by name; no component imports it.
- New: `CodeBlock`, code or a command in the theme's monospace font on a muted surface, with a caption and a copy button; long lines scroll sideways (or wrap with `wrap`) and an overflowing block is a keyboard tab stop.
- New: `ColorSchemeToggle`, the light, dark and system switch (an icon button with a menu, or segmented), with `ColorSchemeProvider`, `useColorScheme` and `colorSchemeScript` (lib `color-scheme` and `color-scheme-script`), which keep the choice and set it before the first paint.
- New: `CopyButton`, a button that copies a value to the clipboard, [says "Copied" for two seconds and announces it once to screen readers](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/copy-button/copy-button.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/copy-button/copy-button.test.tsx::CopyButton > puts the value on the clipboard, says Copied once, calls onCopy and reads Copy again after two seconds)"); it falls back to a text selection where the Clipboard API is unavailable.
- Focus stays in the page when a control leaves it: Alert, Attachment and Toast hand focus to what now stands where they stood (the next control, or the one before), also when an alert's action removes it; Pagination's Previous and Next at an end are `aria-disabled`, not `disabled`, so they keep focus (a test that checked `toBeDisabled()` in Vitest checks `aria-disabled="true"` now). When the last toast goes, focus returns to where it was before it entered the toasts, or stays on the page, never jumping to the page's last control.
- [Forced colours (Windows contrast themes) keep every control's edge and state](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/test/conformance/forced-colors.test.tsx "verify:testExists(test/conformance/forced-colors.test.tsx::forced colours at $width px > $id keeps every control's edge, state and focus, closed and with each overlay open)"): SegmentedControl, Collapsible's trigger, Reorderable's handle, NavigationMenu's triggers, Pagination, Breadcrumb's more button, SwipeActions' actions, Sidebar's rows, Dialog's close button and the bare Dialog, Sheet and Popover trigger and close parts draw a border only forced colours show; Tabs' and SegmentedControl's selected item takes the system's Highlight; MessageList, Carousel, ResizablePanel and Sheet's body outline themselves while they are a tab stop. [The highlighted item of Menu, Select, Combobox and Command, Calendar's chosen days and Pagination's current page take the system's Highlight too, and the days inside a Calendar range a border](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/test/conformance/forced-colors.test.tsx "verify:testExists(test/conformance/forced-colors.test.tsx::forced colours at $width px > $id keeps every control's edge, state and focus, closed and with each overlay open)").
- New: `ImageCropper`, a dialog that crops a photo to a rectangle of `aspect` or a circle: drag or the arrow keys move it the way they point, the slider or Plus and Minus zoom it, and Save hands the cropped image to `onCrop` as a `File`. When the browser cannot open the image or save the crop, it shows and announces why (`labels.openError`, `labels.saveError`) and calls `onError` with `"open"` or `"save"`.
- New: `ImageField`, one photo shown large with Choose photo, Change photo and Remove; with `crop` a chosen photo is cropped first, and the photo stays in a real file input so a plain form submits it. A file of the wrong type, over `maxSize` or that the browser cannot open as a photo (`"unreadable"`) is turned away, keeping what showed, and a status region announces why.
- New: `InstallPrompt`, a quiet offer to install the web app where the browser can install it, [never while it runs installed](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/lib/ouro/use-install-prompt.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/lib/ouro/use-install-prompt.test.tsx::useInstallPrompt > is installed, and offers nothing, while the app runs standalone)") and [not again for two weeks after Not now](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/lib/ouro/use-install-prompt.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/lib/ouro/use-install-prompt.test.tsx::useInstallPrompt > puts the offer away on dismiss(), and keeps it away for remindAfterDays)"); with the `useInstallPrompt()` hook for an offer of your own.
- New: `LocalDate`, a date or time in the reader's own time zone and language, in a `<time>` with the exact instant; the server writes it in UTC and en-US (unless `locale` is set), naming the zone after a time, and the browser in the reader's own zone and language, without a hydration mismatch, and [a calendar date with no time (`"2026-06-01"`) stays that day for every reader](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/local-date/local-date.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/local-date/local-date.test.tsx::dateText > treats only a whole calendar date as one)").
- Fixed: [`MessageList` reads `innerText` only for a message it reads out, not for every message on each change, so a user's message, a streamed token or a message in a closed panel forces no layout](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.test.tsx::MessageList > reads innerText only for a message it reads out, not for a user's message or a streamed token)"); [it also reads nothing out while it is inside a `hidden` element such as a closed panel, where its live region is not heard](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.test.tsx::MessageList > reads out nothing while it is in a closed panel, without laying out what the panel hides, and reads on once shown)"); [scroll bodies that become a tab stop when they overflow (`DialogContent`, `SheetContent`, `Popover`, `ResizablePanel`, `MessageList`) read their size after the browser's layout instead of forcing one on every content change](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/lib/ouro/use-keyboard-scrollable.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/lib/ouro/use-keyboard-scrollable.test.tsx::reads the sizes after the browser's layout, not when the content changes)").
- The test kit's `expectMotion` (`lib/ouro/testing/motion.ts`) [no longer fails when the browser restarts a transition or CSS animation and the restarted one runs its whole course between two frames](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/test/motion.test.ts "verify:testExists(test/motion.test.ts::expectMotion > waits for a transition restarted and ended between two reads) verify:testExists(test/motion.test.ts::expectMotion > waits for a CSS animation restarted and ended between two reads)"), as it can on a slow frame. It listens for the part's transition and animation events while it waits and accepts a replacement only for the same property (an animation of the same name) on the same element that began after the cancellation and ended without being cancelled itself; [a transition cancelled with nothing, or with another property, in its place still fails](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/test/motion.test.ts "verify:testExists(test/motion.test.ts::expectMotion > fails when what ran and ended between two reads in a cancelled transition's place is another property)").
- Fixed: Navbar's overflow tests [use labels too long to fit at 768px in any font](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/navbar/navbar.test.tsx "verify:fileLine(registry/ouro/components/ouro/navbar/navbar.test.tsx, /Long enough not to fit at 768px in any app's font/)"), so they pass in an app whose theme sets a narrower font.
- Fixed: NavigationMenu's current-page tests [read the page link's mark once its colour transitions have finished](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/navigation-menu/navigation-menu.test.tsx "verify:fileLine(registry/ouro/components/ouro/navigation-menu/navigation-menu.test.tsx, /await Promise\.allSettled\(current\.getAnimations\(\)\.map\(\(animation\) => animation\.finished\)\)/) verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/navigation-menu/navigation-menu.test.tsx::NavigationMenu current-page marks > marks only the page link once a pointer that rested on it has moved to open a panel)"), so a pointer left resting on the link by an earlier test no longer fails them while its hover background fades out.
- [OtpField's slots narrow to fit a column too narrow for them (eight slots in a phone's grid column), instead of spilling out of it](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/otp-field/otp-field.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/otp-field/otp-field.test.tsx::OtpField looks > narrows its slots to fit a column too narrow for them, rather than spilling out of it)").
- New: `ouro-census`, the census journey kit: a Playwright spec of yours lists the pages and the states a person can open on them, and the census [holds the exact set of visible controls in each to a committed baseline](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/scripts/kits/census-page.test.mjs "verify:testExists(scripts/kits/census-page.test.mjs::censusTests > fails a state whose page answers another status, redirects, or differs from the baseline)"), [fails an enrolled journey on any control that is not an Ouro control](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/scripts/kits/census-update.test.mjs "verify:testExists(scripts/kits/census-update.test.mjs::checkCensusState > fails an enrolled journey on any unmarked control or unknown item, even when the baseline matches)"), and [lets the unmarked count only fall](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/scripts/kits/census-update.test.mjs "verify:testExists(scripts/kits/census-update.test.mjs::the unmarked ratchet > refuses a rise in one state even when another state of the journey falls by as much)").
- New: `ouro-source-rules`, the hand-rolled-control source rule: it finds the controls an app still builds by hand (raw elements, handlers on plain elements, other UI libraries, control-state styling) and [every component that wraps one](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/scripts/kits/source-rules.test.mjs "verify:testExists(scripts/kits/source-rules.test.mjs::the ratchet against real changes > a wrapper does not launder a control (NiceLink)) verify:testExists(scripts/kits/source-rules.test.mjs::the ratchet against real changes > counts every hop of a wrapper chain, and terminates on a cycle)"), [held to a committed baseline that may only shrink](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/scripts/kits/source-rules.test.mjs "verify:testExists(scripts/kits/source-rules.test.mjs::main > refuses to write a rise against the base ref without a reason)"), with a ratchet against a base ref for pull requests. [The ratchet reads the base's own settings, so a pull request cannot move the baseline or narrow what is read without a recorded reason](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/scripts/kits/source-rules.test.mjs "verify:testExists(scripts/kits/source-rules.test.mjs::main > --base: the settings at the base ref > reads the settings and the baseline at the base's own paths, and fails a moved baseline) verify:testExists(scripts/kits/source-rules.test.mjs::main > --base: the settings at the base ref > records a narrowing with a reason for this pull request, and never honours one already on the base)").
- New: `PageHeader`, the top of a page: its `<h1>` title with an eyebrow, a description, actions beside it (under it on phones), and an optional breadcrumb and image above.
- New: `RelativeTime`, ["3 days ago", "in 5 minutes" or "now"](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/relative-time/relative-time.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/relative-time/relative-time.test.tsx::RelativeTime > says %ims from now as %s: %s)") in the reader's language, kept current on one shared clock (the server writes the UTC date in en-US unless `locale` is set, and hydration matches it), in a `<time>` titled with the full local date and time; [a calendar date with no time (`"2026-06-02"`) counts whole days from the reader's own today ("tomorrow") and is titled with that day](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/relative-time/relative-time.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/relative-time/relative-time.test.tsx::RelativeTime > counts a calendar date in whole days of the reader's calendar, titled with that day)").
- Right to left: Dialog's header leaves room for the close button on the side it is on, SegmentedControl's thumb sits under the pressed segment, and vertical Tabs put the indicator at the inline end. Switch's thumb slides toward the inline end, so in a right-to-left page it stays inside the track when on.
- New: `RouteProgress`, the thin bar across the top of the screen while the app loads the next page or submits a form: it [shows only once a navigation has run 150ms](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/route-progress/route-progress.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/route-progress/route-progress.test.tsx::RouteProgress > waits 150ms before it shows or speaks, so a fast navigation shows and says nothing)"), [creeps toward 90% while it runs](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/route-progress/route-progress.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/route-progress/route-progress.test.tsx::RouteProgress > trickles toward 90%, more slowly the nearer it gets, and never reaches it)"), fills and fades out when it ends, and screen readers hear "Loading" once as it shows.
- New: `Separator` takes `label`, a word such as "or" in the middle of a horizontal line; its text is read in place and the lines are hidden from screen readers. Text children now point to `label` in their type error and development message.
- New: `ShareButton`, a button that shares a link through the system's share sheet where there is one and copies it elsewhere, [saying "Link copied" for two seconds and announcing it once](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/share-button/share-button.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/share-button/share-button.test.tsx::ShareButton > copies the link where there is no share sheet, says Link copied once, and reads Share again after two seconds)"); with the `share()`, `canShare()` and `copyText()` helpers for share actions of your own.
- New: `SkipLink`, the "Skip to main content" link that is the first tab stop on a page; it moves focus to the main content without a history entry.
- [Slider and Progress without a `locale` write their value in en-US on the server and while hydrating, then switch to the reader's locale, so server HTML hydrates without a mismatch in any locale](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/slider/slider.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/slider/slider.test.tsx::Slider across locales > renders the same value text on a server in any locale, and shows the reader's once hydrated) verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/progress/progress.test.tsx::Progress across locales > writes the value in en-US on a server in any locale, hydrates it as it is, and shows the reader's once hydrated)") ("$10" on the server is no longer "10 $" in a German reader's hydration). Slider reserves its value's width only once hydrated.
- New: `SyncStatus`, one line that says whether changes are saved, saving, kept offline on the device or refused, with Retry while there is a problem; [screen readers hear only when a problem starts and when it clears](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/sync-status/sync-status.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/sync-status/sync-status.test.tsx::SyncStatus > speaks when a problem starts and when changes are saved again, and not for every save)"), and [the line keeps one height so the page never moves](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/sync-status/sync-status.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/sync-status/sync-status.test.tsx::SyncStatus > keeps one height whichever state it shows, so the page never moves)").
- New: `TabBar`, the bar of tabs at the bottom of a phone screen, with an accessory set apart (Search), the current tab from the path, the room it needs kept at the end of the page, and a step aside below the screen while the on-screen keyboard is up.
- New: `TimerTray`, the timers that are running, paused or finished in one strip, with Go to and Cancel or Dismiss; [it hides itself while none are](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/timer/timer-tray.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/timer/timer-tray.test.tsx::TimerTray > hides itself while no timer is running)"), and [alerts at zero when the timer's own screen is gone](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/timer/timer-tray.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/timer/timer-tray.test.tsx::TimerTray > alerts on its own when the timer's screen is gone, unless silent)").
- New: `Timer`, a countdown with its own Start, Pause, Resume, Cancel and, at zero, Dismiss and Restart; it keeps time by the clock (and, with `timerId`, across reloads and tabs), [chimes and vibrates at zero unless `silent`, and says "Time's up" once to screen readers](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/timer/timer.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/timer/timer.test.tsx::Timer > says, chimes and buzzes once at zero, shows Time's up, then dismisses or restarts) verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/timer/timer.test.tsx::Timer > only says the time is up when silent)").
- New: `useTimer` and `formatDuration` (`lib-use-timer`), the clock-anchored countdown under `Timer`, shared by `timerId` across components and tabs; and `chime`, `createChimeContext` and `buzz` (`lib-feedback`), a short alert sound and vibration [that resolve false, rather than throw, when the device cannot play them](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/lib/ouro/feedback.test.ts "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/lib/ouro/feedback.test.ts::chime > resolves false, without throwing, when Web Audio fails) verify:testExists(registry/ouro/lib/ouro/feedback.test.ts::buzz > returns false where the device cannot vibrate or refuses)").
