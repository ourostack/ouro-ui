# Changes in this release

- Attachment: an icon for the file's kind (a document, a spreadsheet, slides, a PDF, an image, a video, audio, an archive, code), picked from `contentType` and the name's extension, with `kind` to set it and `icon` to replace it.
- Attachment: `onOpen` opens the file in the app, such as in a Lightbox; without `href` the attachment is a button, and with it a plain click stays in the app while a new tab or window still follows the link.
- New: `Autocomplete`, suggestions at the text caret: a trigger character such as `@` or `:` in a `TextArea` or `TextField` opens a list beside the caret, filtered by what follows it; the arrow keys move, Enter or Tab inserts, Escape closes, focus stays in the text (`aria-activedescendant`), the insertion keeps Undo and the field's change handlers, and one field can have several triggers.
- `Avatar`'s `color="colorful"` mixes its hash before it picks a hue, so ids that differ only in their last character, such as sequential directory ids, spread across the eight hues instead of all taking one ([test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/avatar/avatar.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/avatar/avatar.test.tsx::Avatar colors > spreads ids that differ only in their last character across the hues)")). This changes the hue that many names and ids get. Colorful avatars are new in this release, so apps meet them with the new mapping from the start.
- `Avatar`'s presence dot lies over the corner and takes no room: the initials, icon and picture stay in the middle with or without it, and the initials center on their letters (cap height to baseline) in browsers with CSS `text-box`; before, a dot moved the initials up and toward the start ([test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/avatar/avatar.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/avatar/avatar.test.tsx::Avatar presence > keeps the initials in the middle with or without a dot, and the dot off them (%s))")).
- New: `Avatar` takes `presence` with a required `presenceLabel` (a status dot whose shape and tone say available, busy, away, do not disturb, offline or out of office, read after the name), `shape="square"` for teams, channels, apps and bots, and `icon` instead of initials; its initials now take one of eight hues picked from the name (or `idForColor`), from the new `ouro-base` tokens `--avatar-1` to `--avatar-8`, with `color="brand"` or `color="neutral"` to opt out ([docs](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/docs/Avatar.md), [test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/avatar/avatar.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/avatar/avatar.test.tsx::Avatar presence > draws each state in its own shape and tone)")).
- `Command` items take media: an `avatar` (a picture's URL or an `<Avatar>`) or an `icon` before the label, and an `unread` dot or a `mark` (an unread count's `<Badge>`, "Draft") at the end, so a palette that jumps to any chat shows who each one is with and what is new; `unreadLabel` translates what screen readers hear ([docs](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/docs/Command.md), [test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/command/command.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/command/command.test.tsx::Command media > marks an unread command with a bold label, a dot and a spoken label)")).
- New: Composer, a chat message box: text that grows from one line, a send button, actions, a toolbar shown on demand and attachments in one frame with one focus outline. Enter or Ctrl+Enter sends, but not mid-word in an input method, and `sending` and `sendDisabledReason` hold a message back and say why.
- New: TextArea `highlights` marks ranges of its text in place, with a tone and a label that screen readers hear; the marks follow their words through typing, pasting and undo, and `onHighlightsChange` gives them back moved.
- ConversationList: rows can group under `sections` with headers that open and close, and a row can show a `where` after its title, an `icon` before it, a `mark` at the end of its last line and an avatar element (`avatar={<Avatar … />}`).
- ConversationList takes one Tab stop, on the open conversation, and the arrow keys move between its rows and headers; the time ends the first line and the unread dot the last.
- ConversationList's `onSelect` gets the click as its second argument when the handler takes one, so an app can check `event.isTrusted`; a server action still gets only the id.
- Fixed: pressing the trigger of a `Dialog`, `ConfirmDialog`, `Sheet` or `Command`, a `Lightbox` thumbnail, or a `Button` with `aria-haspopup="dialog"` (which opens a dialog the app controls) while the page smooth-scrolls stops the page where it is until the button's click, so the release lands on the button and the dialog opens; before, the release could land on whatever had scrolled under the pointer, and nothing opened ([tests](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/dialog/dialog.test.tsx "verify:fileLine(registry/ouro/components/ouro/dialog/dialog.test.tsx, /opens and leaves the page where the press found it, pressed mid-scroll/) verify:fileLine(registry/ouro/components/ouro/sheet/sheet.test.tsx, /SheetTrigger and a page scroll at the press/) verify:fileLine(registry/ouro/components/ouro/command/command.test.tsx, /Command's trigger and a page scroll at the press/) verify:fileLine(registry/ouro/components/ouro/lightbox/lightbox.test.tsx, /Lightbox's thumbnails and a page scroll at the press/) verify:fileLine(registry/ouro/components/ouro/button/button.test.tsx, /with aria-haspopup=\"dialog\", stops the scroll where/)")). Enter and Space hold it; an arrow key does not.
- Changed: Dialog and DialogPopup now fit their content and the screen by default (`size="auto"`): at least 32rem, so forms and short questions keep their width, and at most 70% of the screen from 40rem to 56rem, so a table or a gallery grows on a big screen, while paragraphs, headings, list items and labels wrap at a readable 65ch instead of widening it; `size="xl"`, `"2xl"` and `"full"` raise the cap to 56rem, 72rem and the viewport, and `sm`, `md` and `lg` still pin 24, 32 and 40rem, so pass `size="md"` to keep the old default ([docs](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/docs/Dialog.md), [test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/dialog/dialog.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/dialog/dialog.test.tsx::centered dialog > follows the screen by default: a wide table makes it %ipx wide on a %ipx screen)")).
- New: Dialog and Lightbox say in development when they mount already open after a press or a key (`{editing && <Dialog open … />}`), since their open and close transitions can't run then; keep them mounted and change `open` ([test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/dialog/dialog.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/dialog/dialog.test.tsx::Dialog dev messages > says once when it mounts already open after a press, since its transitions can't run)")).
- Fixed: a Dialog or Sheet body, and the other scroll containers that become a tab stop while their content overflows, now also do so when it overflows across, such as around a table wider than a phone, so a keyboard can scroll it ([test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/lib/ouro/use-keyboard-scrollable.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/lib/ouro/use-keyboard-scrollable.test.tsx::makes a container a tab stop when its content overflows across, unless it clips across)")).
- New: `EmojiPicker`, a picker for reactions and for putting emoji into text: search by name and keywords, a category bar, frequently used and quick emoji the app supplies, a skin tone choice, a keyboard grid and `renderEmoji` for an app's own emoji art, in a `Popover` that is a bottom sheet on phones.
- Changed: X-ray's bar (`Inspector`) fits a narrow phone: its label stays on one line, and while it names a spotlit item its buttons take a second row. It sits above a `TabBar` that shows, and above `--ouro-inspector-bottom` when the app sets it for chrome of its own.
- New: Inspector `spotlight` points out one item, such as `spotlight="button"`: X-ray scrolls the first one (in the page's `main`, where there is one) to the middle of the screen (at once with reduced motion), draws its outline stronger with its tag placed first, and its bar says which item it shows or that the page has none ([docs](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/docs/Inspector.md), [test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/inspector/inspector.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/inspector/inspector.test.tsx::Inspector > says when the page has no spotlit item, and points one out when it appears)")).
- X-ray (`Inspector`) outlines only what a person can see: a component that is fully transparent or `visibility: hidden` gets no outline until it shows, as an action shown on hover or focus does while hovered or focused ([test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/inspector/inspector.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/inspector/inspector.test.tsx::Inspector > does not outline a component no one can see, and outlines it while hover or focus shows it)")), and a Toaster is outlined around its toasts while it shows one, and not at all while it shows none ([test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/test/inspector-toaster.test.tsx "verify:testExists(test/inspector-toaster.test.tsx::Inspector over a Toaster > outlines a Toaster only while it shows a toast, around its toasts)")).
- Changed: closing Lightbox gives focus to the thumbnail of the image last shown, not the one that opened it, and a controlled Lightbox takes `returnFocus`, which gets the index of the image last shown and returns the element to focus, such as that image's thumbnail in your layout.
- New: Home and End go to Lightbox's first and last image.
- Changed: Lightbox's `fullScreen` viewer dims behind the image in both themes, with the theme's dark palette for its title, position and buttons, and the system's own colours in forced colours.
- Added: Lightbox `layout="mosaic"` lays a chat message's pictures out in one frame, as chat apps do; `fullScreen` opens the viewer across the whole screen; an image's `width` and `height` keep a lone picture's shape, and its `still` frame shows in its thumbnail under reduced motion.
- Changed: Lightbox runs edge to edge on a phone, and zooms an image drawn smaller than its own size with a double-tap, a pinch or a new zoom button (`zoomLabel` names it); zoomed, a swipe moves around the image instead of to the next one.
- `Menu`'s `items` entries take an `icon`, shown before the label at 16px and left out of the item's name, so `Message`'s `menu` shows icons too ([docs](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/docs/Menu.md), [test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/menu/menu.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/menu/menu.test.tsx::Menu items > shows an entry's icon before its label, at 16px, out of its name)")).
- New: Message takes `variant` ("chat", "post" or "reply") for a chat between people, channel posts and thread replies, with `time`, `continued` runs, a `footer`, a post's `subject` and `thread`, an `Avatar` element as `avatar`, and an action bar from `actions` and `menu`.
- New: ThreadSummary, the line under a post with who replied, a button with the reply count that opens the thread, and the last reply's time, or a Reply button before anyone replies.
- MessageList pulls a `continued` message up under the one before it.
- Message loads its action bar's code, with Tooltip, in the browser for a message that has actions or a menu, and Menu's code when a message's menu first opens.
- `MessageList` takes an app's own message components as well as `Message`: mark each one's root `data-ouro-message-list-item` (with `data-role`, `aria-busy`, `data-ouro-message-author` and `data-ouro-message-content` as `Message` sets them), and the list keeps the reader's place among them and announces them, so an app no longer puts `data-ouro="message"`, Ouro's Message's own name, on a component of its own ([docs](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/docs/MessageList.md), [test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.test.tsx::MessageList > keeps the reader's place among an app's own messages marked data-ouro-message-list-item, and announces them as it does Messages)")).
- `MessageList` is one Tab stop with a keyboard of its own: Tab lands on the newest message in view, the arrow keys, Home, End, Page Up and Page Down move between messages with a focus ring, the right arrow goes into the focused message's toolbar or first control, Tab passes over the other messages' controls so the next Tab leaves the list, and `onMessageKeyDown(message, event)` adds an app's own keys on the focused message, such as 1 to 6 for quick reactions ([docs](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/docs/MessageList.md), [test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.test.tsx::MessageList keyboard > is one Tab stop: Tab lands on the newest message, goes through its controls, and leaves the list)")).
- New: `Message` takes `presence` with a required `presenceLabel`, which shows the author's presence as Avatar's dot on the message's avatar and is read as Avatar reads it beside a written name ([docs](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/docs/Message.md), [test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message.test.tsx::Message presence > shows the author's presence on the avatar in every variant, and says it)")).
- New: NavigationRail, the vertical bar of an app's top-level pages on the start edge of a wide screen: icons over labels that wrap between words, the current item with its filled icon, a bar on the rail's edge and `aria-current="page"`, capped counts spoken in full, separators, a header and a footer, a single tab stop with the arrow keys, Home and End, and a More menu for the items that do not fit its height; links go through the router, and buttons switch views in apps without one.
- New: Ouro AX, for the interfaces agents use (CLIs, HTTP APIs, MCP servers): `ouro-ax` and its `ax-*` items, [copy-in TypeScript for Node with no dependencies](scripts/registry.test.mjs "verify:testExists(scripts/registry.test.mjs::Ouro AX items depend on the ax items they import)"). One command definition gives the parser, help, `help --json`, `--json`, exit codes, one-line errors that name the fix and an MCP tool; an action returns a typed outcome, a wait returns a typed wait with a cursor, and work events say who acted for whom. `ax-claims` is the standard as checkable claims, and `ax-xray` checks a CLI, an HTTP API or an MCP server against them from outside, against an inventory the interface generates, calling what other people see only on a proven mock. Guide: docs/guides/ax.md.
- `ouro-theme` is Pearl, the new default look: white and soft greys, ink text (`#1d1d1f`), one clear blue (`#0066cc`, and `#2997ff` in dark mode), a black dark mode with raised surfaces in `#1c1c1e`, and corners at `--radius: 0.75rem`. An app that installed the old `ouro-theme` keeps its colors until it adds `ouro-theme` again.
- Fixed: opening a `Menu` (and `ColorSchemeToggle`), `Popover` (and `DatePicker`), `Select` or `Combobox` no longer scrolls the page on a site with `scroll-behavior: smooth` or `scroll-padding` ([tests](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/menu/menu.test.tsx "verify:fileLine(registry/ouro/components/ouro/menu/menu.test.tsx, /Menu and the page.s scroll/) verify:fileLine(registry/ouro/components/ouro/popover/popover.test.tsx, /Popover and the page.s scroll/) verify:fileLine(registry/ouro/components/ouro/select/select.test.tsx, /Select and the page.s scroll/) verify:fileLine(registry/ouro/components/ouro/combobox/combobox.test.tsx, /Combobox and the page.s scroll/)")); opening from the keyboard scrolled it toward its end, and a `Popover` opened by a press did too. While one is open, the page moves only when the person scrolls it ([test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/lib/ouro/page-scroll-hold.test.ts "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/lib/ouro/page-scroll-hold.test.ts::holdPageScroll > undoes a page scroll while a popup is open, before the next frame paints it) verify:testExists(registry/ouro/lib/ouro/page-scroll-hold.test.ts::holdPageScroll > leaves a scroll that follows the wheel, a touch, the scroll bar, a key nothing handled or a click in the popup)")).
- Fixed: pressing the trigger of a `Menu`, `Popover`, `Select` or `Combobox` while the page smooth-scrolls (to an anchor, say) stops the page where it is at once, so a menu that opens on the press no longer takes its release, over whatever scrolled under the pointer, for a press outside and closes ([tests](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/menu/menu.test.tsx "verify:fileLine(registry/ouro/components/ouro/menu/menu.test.tsx, /stays open and stops the page where it is, pressed mid-scroll/) verify:fileLine(registry/ouro/components/ouro/popover/popover.test.tsx, /stays open and stops the page where it is, pressed as a smooth scroll begins/) verify:fileLine(registry/ouro/components/ouro/select/select.test.tsx, /stays open and stops the page where it is, pressed as a smooth scroll begins/) verify:fileLine(registry/ouro/components/ouro/combobox/combobox.test.tsx, /stays open and stops the page where it is, pressed as a smooth scroll begins/)")).
- New: ReactionBar takes `onMore`, an "Add reaction" button at the end that opens the app's own picker, with `moreOpen` and `moreLabel`.
- ConversationList: the open conversation's row keeps its highlight under the pointer and while pressed instead of turning the muted grey of the other rows ([test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/conversation-list/conversation-list.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/conversation-list/conversation-list.test.tsx::ConversationList > keeps the open conversation highlighted under the pointer)")).
- Kbd: a key that is also an emoji, such as the Return key's ↩ on Apple platforms, draws as monochrome text, never as a colour emoji ([test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/kbd/kbd.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/kbd/kbd.test.tsx::Kbd > draws a key that is also an emoji as text)")).
- Carousel hides Previous and Next when every slide is already in view, such as a single slide ([test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/carousel/carousel.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/carousel/carousel.test.tsx::Carousel navigation > hides the buttons when every slide is in view)")).
- Lightbox: with one image the viewer shows no position ("1 of 1") and no Previous or Next buttons, and its name is the label alone ([test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/lightbox/lightbox.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/lightbox/lightbox.test.tsx::Lightbox viewer > shows no position, Previous or Next for one image)")).
- New: ShowMore folds content of any kind to a height with `maxHeight`, under a fade, and unfolds when keyboard focus moves below the fold.
- Added: the test kit's `commands.doubleTap({ at, gapMs })` taps twice at one point with real touch input in one command, stamping the taps `gapMs` apart in the page's events however late the second arrives, so a double-tap test holds on a busy machine.
- `ThreadSummary` takes a `label` for its button's accessible name, such as "2 replies to Riley's post Settings mockups", so posts' thread buttons no longer share one name; a label that does not start with the button's words says so in development ([docs](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/docs/ThreadSummary.md), [test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/thread-summary.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/thread-summary.test.tsx::ThreadSummary > names its button for screen readers with label, keeping the words it shows)")).
- TimeGrid takes background events, such as a shared calendar's, in a lane of their own beside the person's, and content of the app's own under the header of every day it shows ([test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/time-grid/time-grid.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/time-grid/time-grid.test.tsx::TimeGrid background events > draws background events in a lane at each day's start, beside the person's own)")).
- New: TimeGrid, the day, work week and week calendar grid: events placed by their times in the time zone the app names or the reader's own, overlapping ones side by side, a row for whole-day events, a now line that passes under event text, and a single Tab stop with arrow keys through events in time order.
- New: `Toggle` and `ToggleGroup` take `shape="pill"` (fully rounded, regular-weight text) and `size="xs"` (24px with a mouse, 32px with a 44px touch target on phones), for rows of filter chips such as All, Unread and Mentions ([docs](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/docs/ToggleGroup.md), [test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/toggle/toggle-group.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/toggle/toggle-group.test.tsx::ToggleGroup > passes shape pill and size xs to every item)")).
- New: `Toolbar`, a row of Button commands and Toggle states with one tab stop, arrow keys, Home and End between controls, groups and separators, tooltips with the label and its `Kbd` shortcut, a More menu for the controls that do not fit, and `keepFocus` so a text editor beside it keeps its selection.
