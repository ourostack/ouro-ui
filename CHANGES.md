# Changes in this release

- Changed: [ax-mcp speaks MCP 2026-07-28](registry/ouro/ax/mcp.test.mts "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/ax/mcp.test.mts::2026-07-28: server/discover names the versions, the capabilities and the server, and the results carry resultType, a cache hint and the server's identity)"): a client sends its version in each request's `_meta` and asks `server/discover`, and every result carries `resultType`, the server's identity and, for `server/discover` and `tools/list`, a `ttlMs` and `cacheScope`. A request that names another version is refused with `-32022`, and one missing `_meta` with `-32602`. It still [answers the `initialize` handshake](registry/ouro/ax/mcp.test.mts "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/ax/mcp.test.mts::an older client opens with initialize, is answered in the revision it asked for, and then needs no _meta)") of 2025-11-25, 2025-06-18, 2025-03-26 and 2024-11-05 for older clients.
- Changed: ax-mcp answers an `initialize` for a revision it doesn't know, such as `2026-07-28`, with 2025-11-25 where it [answered 2025-06-18](registry/ouro/ax/mcp.test.mts "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/ax/mcp.test.mts::an older client opens with initialize, is answered in the revision it asked for, and then needs no _meta)"), and refuses a request with no `_meta` that [comes before `initialize`](registry/ouro/ax/mcp.test.mts "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/ax/mcp.test.mts::before initialize an older client's request is refused with how to open, and a 2026-07-28 request is served beside an older client's on one process)"), which it used to serve.
- Changed: [ax-mcp sends no reply for a call the client cancels](registry/ouro/ax/mcp.test.mts "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/ax/mcp.test.mts::a client that cancels a call gets no reply for it: a queued call never runs, a running one is answered with nothing)"), and doesn't start one that was still queued.
- Changed: [ax-mcp is no longer a preview](scripts/ax-mcp-client.test.mjs "verify:testExists(scripts/ax-mcp-client.test.mjs::the official client that probes with server/discover settles on 2026-07-28 against ax-mcp)"): the official TypeScript client reaches it in 2026-07-28 and in each older revision, and the [Ouro AX guide](docs/guides/ax.md#maturity) says how mature each agent door is.
- Faster: [`Chart` with `type` draws its bars, lines and areas as its own SVG and never loads Recharts](scripts/chart-weight.test.mjs "verify:testExists(scripts/chart-weight.test.mjs::a chart drawn with type adds about 15 KB gzip to an app's build, with no recharts in any chunk)"), so it adds about 15 KB gzip to an app and no Recharts chunk, and its plot draws with the page. Its marks carry `data-ouro-chart-*` attributes, not Recharts' `recharts-*` classes; a Recharts chart passed as children, with `ChartTooltip` and `ChartLegend`, works as before.
- New: AgentHandoff hands what a person has just seen to their coding agent, as one line to paste with Copy beside it, a label that names the group and an optional caption and aside ([test](registry/ouro/components/ouro/agent-handoff/agent-handoff.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/agent-handoff/agent-handoff.test.tsx::AgentHandoff > shows the label, the line, the caption and the aside, as a group named by its label)")); a web address in the line can break after each "/" ([test](registry/ouro/components/ouro/agent-handoff/agent-handoff.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/agent-handoff/agent-handoff.test.tsx::AgentHandoff > lets the line break after each slash of a web address, and never between the two of its scheme)")), and Copy goes under the line in a narrow column ([test](registry/ouro/components/ouro/agent-handoff/agent-handoff.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/agent-handoff/agent-handoff.test.tsx::AgentHandoff > puts Copy under the line in a narrow column, and beside it in a wide one)")).
- New: ChatHeader, the bar above an open chat: a one-to-one chat shows the person with their presence, and a group chat its members' faces, "Ana Lind and 3 others" and a count that opens the member list, with an optional back button and actions.
- Fixed: [Ouro Current records a new rule's existing findings in the `ouro-check.json` of the package whose check found them](scripts/current/current.test.mjs "verify:testExists(scripts/current/current.test.mjs::a check vendored in a monorepo's package has its findings recorded in that package's ouro-check.json, the one it reads, not the root's)"). It wrote them to the repository's root, where a check run from a package does not look, so the findings gated the update instead of passing.
- Fixed: the first-run gate counts a progress bar beside the page's main landmark as under way, so a sign-in form's "Please wait" bar next to `<main>` [is no dead end](scripts/check/first-run-units.test.mts "verify:testExists(scripts/check/first-run-units.test.mts::first-run harness: the judges > a progress bar beside the main landmark, as a sign-in form shows while it checks an account, keeps a page from being a dead end, and without it the page is one (#2351))").
- Fixed: signed in, the first-run gate [no longer calls a card the person opened over the app a dead end](scripts/check/first-run-units.test.mts "verify:testExists(scripts/check/first-run-units.test.mts::first-run harness: the judges > signed in, a card the person opened over the app is no dead end, since Escape puts it away; signed out or unknown it still is (#2359))"), such as the account card or Settings, since Escape puts it away.
- New: TypingIndicator, the line under a chat that says who is writing: their faces, pulsing dots and "Ned Land is writing…", as a polite status that keeps one line's height when nobody is.
- Fixed: Message's typing dots keep the text's colour under forced colours, where they vanished into the page.
- Fixed: ItemGroup, ChoiceCard and CodeBlock no longer clip their content to their squircle, so a change inside repaints only its own part.
