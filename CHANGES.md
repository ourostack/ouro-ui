- New: [Ouro check's `giant-text` rule fails a page whose running text is mostly drawn over 24px](scripts/check/check-rules.test.mts "verify:testExists(scripts/check/check-rules.test.mts::Ouro check's design rules > giant-text: mendelow.me's root type, grown with the screen to 24px, fails on a 4K display and not on a laptop, and ouro-base's 125% step passes)"), as when an app grows its root type with the screen and a 4K display draws body text at 27px.
