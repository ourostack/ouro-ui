# Changes in this release

- New: [fragmentFocusScript](docs/fragmentFocusScript.md "verify:fileLine(docs/fragmentFocusScript.md, /fragmentFocusScript/)"), an inline script for the start of `<body>`, keeps a person's focus in Safari on a page opened at a place on it (`#install`) [while a stylesheet loads](site/e2e/fragment-focus.spec.ts "verify:testExists(site/e2e/fragment-focus.spec.ts::fragmentFocusScript > keeps focus as later styles load, on a page opened at a place while its stylesheet loads)"), and after [a link to a place on the page is followed while one loads](site/e2e/fragment-focus.spec.ts "verify:testExists(site/e2e/fragment-focus.spec.ts::fragmentFocusScript > keeps focus as later styles load, after a link to a place on the page is followed while a stylesheet loads, and still goes there)"): Safari otherwise jumps back to that place, and takes focus, each time a stylesheet loads later.
- New: [CodeBlock reads terminal output with `language="ansi"`](registry/ouro/components/ouro/code-block/code-block.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/code-block/code-block.test.tsx::CodeBlock terminal output > styles bold, dim, italic, underline, strikethrough and the basic and bright colors from SGR codes, and drops the codes)"): bold, dim and the basic and bright colors show from its escape codes, [at 4.5:1 or more in light and dark](registry/ouro/components/ouro/code-block/code-block.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/code-block/code-block.test.tsx::CodeBlock terminal output > keeps every color and dim at 4.5:1 on the block in light and dark, with dim text muted beside the rest)"), and [copying gives the text without them](registry/ouro/components/ouro/code-block/code-block.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/code-block/code-block.test.tsx::CodeBlock terminal output > copies the output without its escape codes)").
