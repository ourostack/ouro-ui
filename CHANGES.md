# Changes in this release

- Changed: `Avatar` [shows its picture at full strength the moment it loads, with no fade](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/avatar/avatar.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/avatar/avatar.test.tsx::Avatar > draws a picture already in the cache at full strength on its first frame, with no fade)"): a picture already in the browser's cache is there on the avatar's first frame, where it used to fade in from transparent over 200ms on every mount.
- New: Ouro check's `states` in `ouro-check.json` [checks a view with no address of its own, reached by presses, as a page of its own](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/scripts/check/check.test.mts "verify:testExists(scripts/check/check.test.mts::Ouro check > checks a state a page reaches by presses as a page of its own, and fails one whose press finds nothing)"), such as an open chat or a call.
- Fixed: Ouro check [loads each page afresh in a browser context of its own, so what one page saves reaches no other, so a route in an address's hash no longer stops the run, and keeps a long address's file names short](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/scripts/check/check.test.mts "verify:testExists(scripts/check/check.test.mts::Ouro check > names a page by site and path across several sites, a URL by its host and path, and the home page home)"), and [judges a page once its finite animations have run](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/scripts/check/check.test.mts "verify:testExists(scripts/check/check.test.mts::Ouro check > judges a page once its animations have run, as a person sees it after a moment)"), so a toast fading in no longer reads as low contrast.
- Fixed: Ouro check's `overlap` [leaves out the part of a picture a clipping box cuts away, and a label on an opaque backing of its own](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/test/check-in-page.test.ts "verify:testExists(test/check-in-page.test.ts::findOverlaps > leaves out the part of a picture a clipping box cuts away, and a label on an opaque backing of its own, but not one on a see-through backing)").
- Fixed: ConversationList [draws the row that opens in its accent on the first frame after the click, and drops the row that closes at once; hover and press still ease](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/conversation-list/conversation-list.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/conversation-list/conversation-list.test.tsx::ConversationList > draws the row that opens in its accent on the first frame, and drops the one that closes at once, but eases into hover)").
- Fixed: Ouro check's close-ups [put the element in the middle of the screen, so a bar stuck to the top no longer covers it](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/scripts/check/check.test.mts "verify:testExists(scripts/check/check.test.mts::Ouro check > photographs a finding on the screen, wherever on or off the page it sits)").
- Fixed: Ouro check's design rules after a run on a large site: `nested-frame` [judges only a child with an edge of its own, not an icon or a clear logo](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/test/check-design-in-page.test.ts "verify:testExists(test/check-design-in-page.test.ts::findDesignIssues: nested-frame > judges a picture's corner only where the picture runs to it: a video, a canvas, a background photo, or an opaque or JPEG corner)"); `giant-picture` [leaves a layer out of the flow and a figure's own picture alone](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/test/check-design-in-page.test.ts "verify:testExists(test/check-design-in-page.test.ts::findDesignIssues: giant-picture > passes a page's own banner, squarer pictures, short ones, pictures in a control, layers out of the flow, a figure's own picture, and pictures that lead no item)"); `baked-corner` [finds the clipping frame past a wrapper that draws no box](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/test/check-design-in-page.test.ts "verify:testExists(test/check-design-in-page.test.ts::listCornerMedia > lists pictures, videos and canvases, with the radius of the frame they fill (past a wrapper that draws no box) and the colour behind it)"); `heading-hierarchy` [measures a heading at the largest size its text is drawn at](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/test/check-design-in-page.test.ts "verify:testExists(test/check-design-in-page.test.ts::findDesignIssues: heading-hierarchy > measures each heading at the largest size its shown text is drawn at, a title's span included)").
- Fixed: Ouro check's `long-lines` [counts columns side by side as lines of their own, and a run of short items as a list](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/test/check-in-page.test.ts "verify:testExists(test/check-in-page.test.ts::findSizeIssues: long-lines > counts columns side by side as lines of their own, and a run of short items as a list, not prose)").
- Fixed: Ouro check's `repaint` [names an animation's properties in one order in every browser](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/check/motion-check.ts "verify:fileLine(registry/ouro/check/motion-check.ts, /filter\(\(key\) => !cheap.has\(key\)\).sort\(\)/)").
- New: Ouro check's design rules, [each failing on a page a person found wrong and passing once it is fixed](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/scripts/check/check.test.mts "verify:testExists(scripts/check/check.test.mts::Ouro check's design rules > fails each seed case where a person saw it, with numbers that say why)"): `giant-picture` (a banner leading a content item), `cramped-heading` (a heading with no room above it), `nested-frame` (a double border, or corners that don't nest), `baked-corner` (a recording's own corner inside its frame, read from its pixels), `type-scale`, `shadow-scale`, `grey-on-colour` and `heading-hierarchy`, after Refactoring UI.
- New: Ouro check's `jank` rule [walks each page at one size and theme](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/scripts/check/check.test.mts "verify:testExists(scripts/check/check.test.mts::Ouro check's design rules > fails each seed case where a person saw it, with numbers that say why)"), hovering cards, opening and closing popups, scrolling and panning, and fails a step whose longest frame is over 250 ms or whose every run forces more than 50 layouts or shifts the page; `"walk": false` turns it off.
- New: Ouro check's `https` rule [holds a site on the web to a 301 from `http://` to `https://` and a year of HSTS, and its `www.` name too](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/scripts/check/check.test.mts "verify:testExists(scripts/check/check.test.mts::Ouro check's design rules > https: a site moves http:// to https:// with a 301 and asks for HSTS for a year, and so does its www. name)").
- New: Ouro check's `blurry-image` rule [finds a video drawn larger than its frames](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/check/size-check.ts "verify:fileLine(registry/ouro/check/size-check.ts, /a video drawn/)").
- New: Ouro check's `repaint` rule [finds an endless animation that repaints every frame at rest](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/scripts/check/check.test.mts "verify:testExists(scripts/check/check.test.mts::Ouro check > finds each rule on its fail page and nothing on any pass page, with a report, a contact sheet and close-ups)"): one that animates anything but transform and opacity, or anything inside an SVG drawing.
- Fixed: `EmojiText`, `EmojiPicker`, `ReactionBar` and the other parts that index an emoji catalogue [find a fresh catalogue's index with one weak lookup and a comparison, not a WeakMap per emoji](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/emoji/emoji.index.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/emoji/emoji.index.test.tsx::catalogueIndex > makes no WeakMap per emoji, however many the catalogue holds)"), so a catalogue of a few thousand emoji no longer chains thousands of WeakMaps that WebKit's garbage collector walked a level at a time in every collection, holding the main thread for hundreds of milliseconds; [the 8 catalogues indexed most recently that start with the same emoji are kept](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/emoji/emoji.index.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/emoji/emoji.index.test.tsx::catalogueIndex > keeps the lists of the 8 catalogues indexed most recently that start with the same emoji)").
- Fixed: Ouro check's `image-fade` [reads a linear mask along its line and finds only a picture in a band where it isn't opaque](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/test/check-in-page.test.ts "verify:testExists(test/check-in-page.test.ts::findImageFades > reads a linear mask's ramp along its line and finds only a picture in the part it fades, and any picture under a mask it can't read so)"), so a picture in the middle of a list that fades at its edges, or under a mask that fades nowhere, passes.
- Changed: `Navbar` and `NavigationMenu` [underline the current page in the primary colour](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/navbar/navbar.tsx "verify:fileLine(registry/ouro/components/ouro/navbar/navbar.tsx, /aria-\[current=page\]:underline aria-\[current=true\]:underline decoration-primary decoration-2/)"), as `FamilyBar` and `FamilyFooter` mark the current site, instead of a bar at the start edge that read like a text cursor; [forced colours keep it](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/navbar/navbar.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/navbar/navbar.test.tsx::Navbar current-page marks > keeps its underline in the system palette)").
- Fixed: on a page below a link, `Navbar` and `NavigationMenu` [mark that link, the section, `aria-current="true"` instead of `"page"`](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/navbar/navbar.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/navbar/navbar.test.tsx::Navbar > marks the current page from currentPath, and the section a page under an item is in as current but not the page)"), so a screen reader hears one current page; it keeps the same look.
- Changed: `FamilyBar` [starts its first link's text where `FamilyFooter`'s does](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/family-bar/family-bar.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/family-bar/family-bar.test.tsx::FamilyBar > starts its first link's text where the FamilyFooter's does, at the padding's edge, with the same padding, or after its brand)"), at the padding's edge, with the footer's 1rem padding, so a page's bar, logo and footer share one column edge.
- Fixed: `FamilyFooter`'s [extras fill the rest of the row, or a row of their own, to its end edge](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/family-bar/family-footer.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/family-bar/family-footer.test.tsx::FamilyFooter > lets the site's extras fill the rest of the row, or a row of their own, to its end edge)"), so a site can spread them across it (`me-auto` on the first).
- New: Ouro check's `fades.ts` [finds a wash over a picture too](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/scripts/check/fades.test.mts "verify:testExists(scripts/check/fades.test.mts::the fade check > finds a wash over a picture: a gradient that reaches transparent on a pseudo-element or a positioned layer, in a file with a picture)"): a gradient that reaches transparent on a `::before`, an `::after` or an absolutely positioned layer in a file that draws a picture, or [one named a wash, scrim, fade, veil or haze](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/scripts/check/fades.test.mts "verify:testExists(scripts/check/fades.test.mts::the fade check > finds a gradient named a wash, scrim, fade, veil or haze, with a picture in the file or without: the link-preview card's wash)"), and reads `.mts` and `.cts` files; a sky or a glow with no overlay role passes.
- Fixed: `ShowMore` with `lines` or `maxHeight` [has its "Show more" button and its fold's fade in the first frame the browser paints](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/collapsible/show-more.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/collapsible/show-more.test.tsx::ShowMore in its first frame > has its button and fade in the first frame the browser paints, and nothing for what fits)"): its resize observer starts in a layout effect and commits its answer before paint, where the button and fade used to arrive a frame or more after the folded content.
- Changed: every `ShowMore` [shares one resize observer](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/collapsible/show-more.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/collapsible/show-more.test.tsx::ShowMore in its first frame > measures many folds with one observer, every one's button in the same first painted frame)"), which reads every fold's size before it commits any answer, so a list of many folds lays out once in a frame rather than once per fold.
