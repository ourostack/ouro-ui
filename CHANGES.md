# Changes in this release

- New: an Autocomplete trigger takes `catalogue`, an image emoji catalogue, in place of `items`: it suggests the emoji whose shortcode, name or keyword starts with what was typed, drawn as images, and inserts the `:shortcode:` token, which `EmojiText` draws as the image once a `Composer`'s message is sent. `AutocompleteItem` takes `emoji` to show a catalogue's emoji before its label.
- New: EmojiPicker takes `catalogue`, an image emoji catalogue, in place of `categories`: it draws only the catalogue's images (an organization's custom emoji in a category of their own), searches names, shortcodes and keywords, takes frequently used and quick emoji by id, and gives `onSelect` the emoji's id and its `:shortcode:` token. A category's button now shows its first emoji through `renderEmoji` too.
- Fixed: EmojiPicker's skin tone button and its choices draw the app's emoji through `renderEmoji` (the first emoji with tones), not a hand character.
- New: `Emoji` and `EmojiText`, an image emoji catalogue's emoji: `Emoji` draws one as an `<img>` named by its name, an animated one (an animated image, or a filmstrip of square frames stepped at its `fps`) playing while its button is under the mouse or in focus and still with reduced motion, and `EmojiText` draws a message's `:shortcode:` tokens and emoji text as the catalogue's images. `EmojiItem` and `EmojiCatalogue` describe the catalogue: a standard set and an organization's custom emoji.
- Fixed: `MessageList` keeps a scrolled-up reader's place exactly when older history loads above in the same frame as their own scroll; before, the view jumped by the history's height or took back the reader's scroll. It holds the first message whose top is in view, by where it sits in the content, and leaves the correction to the browser where an app turns `overflow-anchor` back on ([test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.test.tsx::MessageList > keeps the reader's place exactly when their scroll event comes after older history arrives, before the list has kept their place)")).
- `MessageList` messages spring as the reader scrolls, as in a phone's messages app: each trails the scroll a little and catches up on a spring, the farther from the pointer, finger or focused message the more, and a fling or a push past either end spreads them apart for a moment; drawn with `transform` alone, it never moves the scroll position, leaves no transform at rest, moves only the messages near view, and is off with reduced motion or `springy={false}` ([docs](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/docs/MessageList.md), [test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.springs.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.springs.test.tsx::MessageList springs > trails the wheel the more the farther a message is from the pointer, and settles back to no transform)")).
- New: `Moment`, the slot for an empty, loading, error, first-run or success state: art (any node, usually an inline SVG), a title, one line and [up to two actions](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/moment/moment.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/moment/moment.test.tsx::Moment > renders the art, an <h3> title, the line and up to two actions)"), in the page's flow. Its art stops moving with reduced motion, a `dismissKey` keeps a dismissal, `cap` limits how often a success celebrates, and `quiet` (or `MomentProvider`) makes it a compact row.
- New: `ax-voice`, a line of character for a person at a terminal: `defineCli({ voice })` takes `banner`, `done`, `summary` and `empty` lines, printed after the fact, and only when stdout is a terminal without `--json`, `--quiet` or `CI`. `help --json` lists the fixed lines.
- New: `ax-flows`, an adopter's agent flows as data (`defineFlow`, `defineSuite`), run in CI without a model against its proven mock; it writes `findings.jsonl` and `report.json` in the `flow-review/0` format, with an X-ray report attached, and fails on an unknown failure or on a `known` one that now passes.
- New: should claims in the claim set and the X-ray: `cli.voice-off-in-json`, `cli.voice-off-when-piped`, `cli.fact-before-voice`, `cli.audience-named`, `cli.text-refs`, `cli.named-is-listed` and `cli.help-size`; `cli.no-stack-trace` also runs the overview into a closed pipe.
- New: an ambiguous word's fix carries the words and flags the agent gave (`{args}` and `{flags}` in a meaning's `use`), and an unknown flag spelt as one of an argument's `values` (`--week`) gets the word as its fix.
- New: `quietPipes(process)` ends a tool quietly when its reader goes away (`tool help | head -1`), `emptyText()` says what an empty list is empty of and what to run next, and `validateOutcome` warns when an action for other people has a placeholder audience such as "other people" (`isGenericAudience`).
- New: ReactionBar takes `catalogue`, an image emoji catalogue: reactions and quick reactions go by emoji id and show the catalogue's images, named by the emoji's name, an animated one playing while its button is under the mouse or in focus.
- New: a ReactionBar reaction takes `tooltip`, who reacted, shown in a Tooltip on hover and keyboard focus and read as the reaction's description; without it a pressable reaction's tooltip is its name.
- New: the test kit's `commands.pointerPress({ selector, action: "click" })` presses and releases the real mouse in one command, so the release follows the press however loaded the runner is, and nothing scrolls the element into view first.
- Added: the test kit's `pressDuringScroll(selector, { from, to, scroll })` and `pressAndLetGo(selector)` (`testing/press.ts`, with their own tests in `press.test.ts`) press the real mouse during a smooth page scroll and let go with the page's clock held from the press until the release lands, so a test of an overlay pressed mid-scroll holds on a busy machine.
- Fixed: an `autoGrow` `TextArea` leaves what scrolls around it where it was as it measures itself, so editing the newest message in place at the end of a `MessageList` keeps the list at the end, with no "Jump to latest"; before, each line it grew pulled the list up, as the textarea collapses for a moment to measure and the browser scrolled the list back into range ([test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/text-field/text-area.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/text-field/text-area.test.tsx::TextArea autoGrow > leaves what scrolls around it where it was as it grows a line, at the end of a scrolling list)")).
