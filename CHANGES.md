# Changes in this release

- New: `Popover`, `Tooltip` and `HoverCard`, portalled at `--ouro-layer` like Dialog and Sheet. `Popover` takes `trigger`, or `open` with `onOpenChange` and `anchor`; a required `title` (`hideTitle`); `side` and `align`; and `presentation="auto"`, which makes it a bottom sheet below 640px. `Tooltip` takes `content` with its trigger as the child, never opens after a touch, and warns in development when its content holds essential information. `HoverCard` previews a link on devices that can hover, and nowhere else.
- `OuroProvider` now groups tooltips (Base UI's `Tooltip.Provider`): once one shows, its neighbours open instantly. `ouro-lib` therefore depends on `@base-ui/react`, which every component already installs.
- New: `Progress`, a labeled progress bar: determinate with a number `value`, or indeterminate with `value={null}`, which screen readers hear as `indeterminateText` ("Loading" by default); a required `label`. Under reduced motion the indeterminate bar pulses instead of sliding.
- New: `Skeleton` (`shape`: `rect`, `text`, `circle`), a placeholder hidden from screen readers. Under reduced motion it is a flat fill that pulses its opacity instead of shimmering.
- New: `Spinner` (`size`, `label`, `tone`), a loading status with a hidden label. Under reduced motion it pulses its opacity instead of spinning.
- New: `Toaster` and `toast()`. Render `<Toaster />` once, then call `toast("Saved", { tone, description, action, duration })` from anywhere (`toast` is in `lib/ouro/toast`, installed with every component). Up to three toasts show; later ones wait their turn, a `destructive` toast ahead of the others, and each one's countdown starts when it shows. Toasts pause on hover and focus, dismiss with a swipe, and are announced once: politely, or assertively for `destructive`. A toast with an `action` stays until it is dismissed unless you pass a `duration`.
- `ouro-base` adds the `ouro-progress` and `ouro-shimmer` keyframes. The `ouro-testing` kit adds a `touchDrag` command for real touch swipes in Chromium.
