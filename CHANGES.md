# Changes in this release

- Added: Ouro AX: an agent action declared `scope: "self"` is one an agent takes only on its own item; `bindAx` needs its twin to say who is calling with `--agent <me>` ([test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/ax/component.test.mts "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/ax/component.test.mts::a self-scoped action is an agent's, and bindAx needs its twin to say who is calling)")), and the twin's command declares `self: { owner }`, takes `--agent <you>` and is refused (`not-yours`) before `plan()` or `run()` on an item that is not the caller's, naming whose it is and what to run instead, in the CLI and over MCP ([test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/ax/cli.test.mts "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/ax/cli.test.mts::a self-scoped command runs on the calling agent's own item and refuses another agent's before plan() or run(), naming both)")).
- Added: Ouro AX: the X-ray's must claim `ui.self-scope-refuses` runs a self-scoped twin as an agent that owns nothing and fails one that acts; the claim set and the X-ray are at 1.5.0 ([test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/ax/xray.test.mts "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/ax/xray.test.mts::a self-scoped twin that does not check the owner fails ui.self-scope-refuses, with what it saw)")).
- Emoji: an animated emoji fetches its animation once its still image has loaded, at low priority, so the first play under the mouse or in focus starts at once; not for an emoji given `fetchPriority="low"` (EmojiPicker's emoji further down its grid), while the browser asks to save data, or with reduced motion, which plays nothing.
- Fixed: Inspector (X-ray): [a component that hides as its transition ends loses its outline in WebKit too, which can read the element's style from before the transition ended in the frame it ends; X-ray measures once more the frame after anything last moved, then rests](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/inspector/inspector.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/inspector/inspector.test.tsx::Inspector > measures once more the frame after motion ends, as WebKit can read the old style in the frame a transition ends, then rests)").
- Added: Menu: `anchor`, a menu the app opens itself, at an element, a measured box or a point, named by its `aria-label` and putting focus back on `returnFocus` when it closes, so many rows can share one menu ([test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/menu/menu.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/menu/menu.test.tsx::Menu anchor > opens at a point the app gives, named by its aria-label, and puts focus back where the app says when it closes)")); beside an element it is a bottom sheet on a phone, as a dropdown is ([test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/menu/menu.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/menu/menu.test.tsx::Menu anchor > opens beside an element, as a bottom sheet below 640px, and at a point as a menu)")).
- MessageList: [a list the reader scrolled up in that takes a page of older messages and newer ones in one commit renders no more than the 60 children it drew before and the 20 newest](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/test/message-list-windowing.bench.test.tsx "verify:testExists(test/message-list-windowing.bench.test.tsx::MessageList older page > renders about a fifth of the rows, not the list again, when a short list the reader scrolled up in takes 200 older rows and 360 newer in one commit)"), where it drew the whole list once more; new `heightCache`, a map from the app that [keeps each row's height by key and width, so a list that mounts again with it is as tall from its first commit as it was](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.windowing.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.windowing.test.tsx::MessageList windowing > keeps the height of every row in a heightCache the app hands in, so a list that mounts again with it is as tall, in its first commit, as it was)").
- MessageList: [a conversation of more than 60 children draws only the messages near its view](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.windowing.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.windowing.test.tsx::MessageList windowing > draws only the messages near the view of a long conversation, in a list as tall as all of them)"), each measured as it draws, so a long conversation opens and redraws in about the time a screenful does; [the reader's place holds as messages above are measured](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.windowing.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.windowing.test.tsx::MessageList windowing > keeps the reader's place while messages above are measured, scrolling up through them)") and [as a page of older messages loads, which goes in at once](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.windowing.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.windowing.test.tsx::MessageList windowing > keeps the reader's place when a page of older messages loads above, putting it in at once)"); [Home and End reach the first and newest message, drawing them](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.windowing.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.windowing.test.tsx::MessageList windowing > moves focus to the first message with Home and back to the newest with End, drawing the message it reaches)"); and [`windowing={false}` keeps the whole conversation in the page](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.windowing.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.windowing.test.tsx::MessageList windowing > keeps every message in the page with windowing={false})"). The browser's find in page sees only the messages drawn.
- MessageList: new `actionsRef`, whose [`reveal(id)` shows a message the list has not drawn](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.windowing.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.windowing.test.tsx::MessageList windowing > reveals a message that is not drawn, by its id, in the middle of the view, and focuses it when asked)") (by a child's `id` or its `key`, [resolving once the message is in place](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.windowing.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.windowing.test.tsx::MessageList windowing > reveals a row component that takes no id by its key, and opens at one by its key, resolving once it is in place)")), for a quote, a link to a message or find in chat, and whose [`jumpToNewest()` goes to the end and follows new messages again](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.windowing.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.windowing.test.tsx::MessageList windowing > jumps to the newest message from far up, and follows new messages again)").
- MessageList: [its children now sit in elements of their own that draw no box (`display: contents`, `data-ouro-message-list-slot`)](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.windowing.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/message/message-list.windowing.test.tsx::MessageList windowing > puts each child in a slot that draws no box, keeping a continued message's spacing, in a list of any length)"), so CSS that selected the direct children of `[data-ouro-message-list-content]` selects those elements now: select `[data-ouro-message-list-slot] > *` instead.
- New: `portholeView` (`lib-porthole-view`) decides what Porthole, the status spot, shows from a service's JSON snapshot, its heartbeat and the clock: [one item at a time, an action over an error over working over rest](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/lib/ouro/porthole-view.test.ts "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/lib/ouro/porthole-view.test.ts::portholeView > shows an action over an error, an error over working and working over rest)"), ["Updated just now" only while every area is fresh, else the real age](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/lib/ouro/porthole-view.test.ts "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/lib/ouro/porthole-view.test.ts::portholeView > at rest, says the real age of the area verified longest ago once any area is past fresh)"), ["Status unknown" once the heartbeat stops](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/lib/ouro/porthole-view.test.ts "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/lib/ouro/porthole-view.test.ts::portholeView > a stopped heartbeat outranks every item, an action too, which waits in the open lines)"), and `nextChangeAt`, the one moment to look again; `portholeStatus` gives the same view as versioned JSON for an agent or a CLI ([guide](https://ui.ouro.bot/docs/porthole-snapshot/)).
- Changed: ShowMore folds with CSS in its first commit and guesses nothing about overflow, so a message in a list no longer moves when the browser has measured it: [no button as React commits it](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/collapsible/show-more.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/collapsible/show-more.test.tsx::ShowMore in its first frame > with %s, has no button as React commits it, because it guesses nothing about overflow)"), then [one observer pass adds the button to what overflows without moving a box](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/collapsible/show-more.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/collapsible/show-more.test.tsx::ShowMore in its first frame > does not move short content or the box of long content when the browser has measured it)"); `estimate` is no longer read and is kept only so an app that passes it still compiles.
- `ouro-source-rules`: [a new category, `gated-load`, counts a data or asset load that waits for focus, visibility, the person's return, idle time or scrolling into view (a `visibilitychange`, focus, blur or pageshow listener, a `document.hidden` or `hasFocus()` condition, `requestIdleCallback`, an `IntersectionObserver` or an in-view or idle hook, an element's `loading="lazy"`, or a `src` that waits on one), and its message says to load eagerly, newest first; rendering-only gates and saving on hide pass](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/scripts/kits/source-rules.test.mjs "verify:testExists(scripts/kits/source-rules.test.mjs::gated-load > lets rendering-only gates through: an animation or caret paused, what is drawn windowed, a media stream)"). After updating the kit, record the loads an app already gates with `--write-baseline --allow-regression <reason>`, and from there the count can only fall.
- Changed: in a day, TimeGrid's lane of background events [takes 40% of the day at any width](registry/ouro/components/ouro/time-grid/time-grid.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/time-grid/time-grid.test.tsx::TimeGrid background events > gives a day's lane 40% of a wide day, past 32rem, so a wide window has room to label each event)"), at least 7rem. Before, a fixed cap kept it narrow, so in a wide window overlapping events cut their titles short beside a much wider column of the person's own.
