# Changes in this release

- New: Ouro check's first-run harness (scripts/ouro/check/first-run.ts) drives an installed Mac app from a fresh machine to signed in and synced through the detours a person takes, dismissing dialogs, pressing early, quitting, going offline and signing out, and fails a blank pane, a dead end, a dismissed required step or a signed-out empty state.
- Fixed: a Dialog or a Sheet opened from inside a Sheet [shows above it](registry/ouro/components/ouro/dialog/dialog.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/dialog/dialog.test.tsx::Dialog stacking above the app's fixed bars > shows above a Sheet it is opened from, modal %s)") ([a Sheet too](registry/ouro/components/ouro/sheet/sheet.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/sheet/sheet.test.tsx::sheet in a sheet > shows a Sheet opened from inside a Sheet above it, outer modal %s)")). Its portal renders in the outer sheet's, under that sheet's own layers, as popups did before #1983; it now stacks above them there, and stays at `--ouro-layer` on the page. Give-back from an app whose side panel lifted its dialogs with CSS of its own.
