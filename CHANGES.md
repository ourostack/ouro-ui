# Changes in this release

- Added: `Sheet` takes `scrim`, a light dimming layer behind a `modal={false}` sheet that [takes no presses, so a press on the page still reaches it and closes the sheet with `closeOnOutsidePress`](registry/ouro/components/ouro/sheet/sheet.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/sheet/sheet.test.tsx::non-modal sheet > with scrim, the page keeps its colours and contrast, the layer takes no presses, and a press on the page still closes it)"); its color and strength are the new `--ouro-scrim-color` and `--ouro-scrim-max` tokens, and the `SheetScrim` part draws it.
- Fixed: a non-modal `Sheet`'s `scrim` no longer tints the page. The page beside a non-modal sheet is in use, and the tint lowered the contrast of text under it, enough to fail an app whose text sat near the line (the "Change look" button in Spoonjoy). The layer still passes presses through, and the sheet's hairline and shadow set it apart. `--ouro-scrim-max` is gone ([test](registry/ouro/components/ouro/sheet/sheet.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/sheet/sheet.test.tsx::non-modal sheet > with scrim, the page keeps its colours and contrast, the layer takes no presses, and a press on the page still closes it)")).
