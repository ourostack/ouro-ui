# Changes in this release

- New: Ouro AX: `ax-component` declares a component's agent side, [what an agent reads from it and each action's effect, with `agent: true` or a `personOnly` reason, never both or neither](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/ax/component.test.mts "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/ax/component.test.mts::an action is agent or person-only, exactly one, and every problem throws one line with its fix)"), and `bindAx` [binds each read and agent action to its CLI twin and returns the X-ray inventory's `ui` entries](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/ax/component.test.mts "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/ax/component.test.mts::bindAx needs a twin for every read and agent action, refuses one for a person-only action, and returns the inventory entries)").
- New: Ouro AX: `defineCli({ personOnly })` declares the words only the person acts on, which [refuse with the reason and how the person does it, as a refused outcome under `--json`](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/ax/cli.test.mts "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/ax/cli.test.mts::a person-only word refuses with its reason and how the person does it, as a refused outcome under --json)"), and [`help --json` lists them under `personOnly`](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/ax/help.test.mts "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/ax/help.test.mts::person-only words are listed in the overview and in help --json, with the reason and how the person does it)").
- New: Ouro AX: claim set 1.2.0 and ax-xray 1.2.0 [check a `ui` inventory: every twin is a command with the action's effect, person-only words refuse, a read of what people say answers `needs-human` until the person asks the agent to follow, its stream refuses every line without a words trigger, and a read resumes from `--cursor`](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/ax/xray.test.mts "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/ax/xray.test.mts::the calling components' twins conform: each twin is listed with its effect, person-only words refuse, and listening reads wait for a follow)"); [a CLI with no `ui` inventory has no `ui` surface checked](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/ax/xray.test.mts "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/ax/xray.test.mts::the calling components' twins conform: each twin is listed with its effect, person-only words refuse, and listening reads wait for a follow)").
- New: Ouro AX: [`RESERVED_PERSON_ONLY` keeps the person's microphone, camera and screen, answering, joining and declining a call, admitting from the lobby and recording person-only in every component](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/ax/component.test.mts "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/ax/component.test.mts::a reserved action is always the person's: declaring it agent: true throws with its reason)"), and [`expectAxRendered(ax, container)` checks that a component renders `data-ax-action="<id>"` on each declared action's control](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/ax/component.test.mts "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/ax/component.test.mts::expectAxRendered finds each declared action's control by data-ax-action, and names the ones it misses)").
- New: Ouro AX: [reserved person-only actions are matched by meaning, so aliases such as `mic-on`, `share-screen`, `accept`, `start-recording`, a bare `mic`, any toggle such as `toggle-mute`, `join-meeting` and `admit-all` are the person's too, while `mute`, `camera-off`, `stop-share` and call verbs away from a call, such as `share-link` and `answer-message`, stay an agent's](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/ax/component.test.mts "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/ax/component.test.mts::reserved ids are matched by meaning: every alias and case is the person's, and what reduces exposure stays an agent's)"); [a component imports the kit as `@/registry/ouro/ax/component`, which installs as the app's lib alias](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/scripts/install-check.test.mjs "verify:testExists(scripts/install-check.test.mjs::an installed file must equal its source once import paths are mapped to the app's aliases)").
- `Combobox` items take `media` (an `Avatar`, shown on the row and on the chip), `description` (a second, muted line, cut to one line and whole on hover) and `meta` (a mark such as a `Badge` at the row's end), so it can pick people ([test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/combobox/combobox.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/combobox/combobox.test.tsx::Combobox rich items > shows media, a description and a mark on a row, and media on its chip)")).
- `Combobox` takes `autoHighlight`, so Enter picks the first match; `onInputKeyDown`, which hears each key before Combobox and can stop its handling with `preventDefault()`; `inputValue`, the text when the app keeps it; and `readOnly` ([test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/combobox/combobox.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/combobox/combobox.test.tsx::Combobox keys > hands the app each key first, and leaves a key the app prevents alone)")).
- `Combobox`'s list sits 1000 above `--ouro-layer`, as Select's does, so inside a `Dialog` it shows over the dialog instead of behind it ([test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/combobox/combobox.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/combobox/combobox.test.tsx::Combobox in a Dialog > draws its list above the dialog it sits in)")).
- An icon or picture inside a `Combobox` item keeps its own size; only the check before a picked item is drawn at 16px ([test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/combobox/combobox.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/combobox/combobox.test.tsx::Combobox rich items > shows media, a description and a mark on a row, and media on its chip)")).
- Changed: when Command's results change while the search stays the same (results from a slow source), [the highlight stays on the same command](registry/ouro/components/ouro/command/command.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/command/command.test.tsx::Command results that stream in > keeps the highlight on the same command when results arrive above it)"), by its new `value` (its label by default), and goes to the first command when that one has gone.
- New: Command says how many results there are, [politely and once after they settle](registry/ouro/components/ouro/command/command.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/command/command.test.tsx::Command results that stream in > says how many results there are, politely and once, after they settle)"), in words from `resultsLabel`.
- New: a command with `restoreFocus: false` [leaves focus where its `onSelect` puts it](registry/ouro/components/ouro/command/command.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/command/command.test.tsx::Command and focus after a choice > returns focus to its trigger after a choice, and leaves it where the choice put it with restoreFocus false)") when the palette closes, instead of returning it to the trigger.
- New: a command's `description` is a second line under its label, which the search matches too, and Command's `highlightMatches` [marks the words of the search](registry/ouro/components/ouro/command/command.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/command/command.test.tsx::Command match highlighting > marks the words of the search in each command's label and description, and keeps the text as it was)") in both, or where a command's `highlight` ranges say, in colours that [keep their contrast in light, dark and forced colours](registry/ouro/components/ouro/command/command.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/command/command.test.tsx::Command match highlighting > draws marked words that keep their contrast in light and dark, highlighted or not, and in forced colours)").
- New: `Emoji` takes `placeholder`, an image's address or an element, which [shows in the emoji's own box until its picture has loaded and decoded](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/emoji/emoji.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/emoji/emoji.test.tsx::Emoji placeholder > shows a placeholder image in the emoji's own box until the picture has decoded, then the picture, named as before, with no shift)"); the picture then [fades in over `--ouro-duration-fast`, at once with reduced motion](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/emoji/emoji.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/emoji/emoji.test.tsx::Emoji placeholder > fades the picture in and the placeholder out over the short duration, and swaps them at once with reduced motion)") or [under `<OuroProvider motion="reduced">`](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/emoji/emoji.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/emoji/emoji.test.tsx::Emoji placeholder > fades by OuroProvider's motion over the device's: over the short duration under full, and at once under reduced)"), and [a picture that fails to load or decode leaves the placeholder showing](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/emoji/emoji.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/emoji/emoji.test.tsx::Emoji placeholder > keeps the placeholder for a picture that fails to load or to decode, and brings it back for a new picture that fails)"). With it, the emoji's root is a `<span>` around the `<img>`, and `className` goes on the `<span>`.
- New: `EmojiPicker` with a `catalogue` takes `placeholder` and [passes it to every emoji it draws](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/emoji-picker/emoji-picker.catalogue.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/emoji-picker/emoji-picker.catalogue.test.tsx::EmojiPicker with a catalogue > passes its placeholder to every emoji it draws, each picture keeping its emoji's name, until each picture has decoded)").
- Installs from GitHub take a release tag, `npx shadcn@latest add ourostack/ouro-ui/<item>#v<version>`, or go through the `@ouro` namespace (`@ouro/<item>`): [`main` no longer commits `registry.json`, so an address without a tag, or with a branch or a commit, stops with "did not return a root registry.json file"](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/scripts/registry.test.mjs "verify:testExists(scripts/registry.test.mjs::main commits no registry.json, and a release commit adds it, so only a release tag installs from GitHub)").
- `lib-claims`: [the claims check lints a generated surface (`generated` in `claims.config.json`) even when git ignores it](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/scripts/claims.test.mjs "verify:testExists(scripts/claims.test.mjs::a generated surface git ignores is still linted)").
- Fix: a dropdown `Menu` closes when focus moves anywhere outside it, as it does on a click outside. Left open, the focus guard Base UI keeps before its trigger sent a forward Tab back to what Tab was leaving, so Tab stuck once on the control before the trigger ([test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/menu/menu.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/menu/menu.test.tsx::Menu focus > closes when focus moves outside it, so a forward Tab from before its trigger goes on to the trigger)")).
- `Moment`'s art carries `data-moment-art`, with the new `artName` prop as its value ("waving"), and `Celebrate` carries `data-celebrate` while it celebrates, so checkers and tests find the art and the celebration without marking them by hand ([test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/moment/moment.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/moment/moment.test.tsx::Moment > marks its art with data-moment-art, carrying artName)")).
- `Moment` takes `size="hero"`, with art up to 220px tall for the main state of a large, otherwise empty pane, holding at 96px in a pane narrower than 24rem ([test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/moment/moment.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/moment/moment.test.tsx::Moment sizes > gives hero art 220px, and 96px in a pane narrower than 24rem)")).
- `Moment`'s art has a motion budget: its looping animations settle at the end of their turn about 5 s after it appears (its SVG animations pause at their start), and with a `dismissKey` or `id` its entrance plays once a session, so a moment that comes back as people switch views arrives still ([test](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/moment/moment.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/moment/moment.test.tsx::Moment motion budget > settles the art's loops at the end of their turn within 5 s, SVG animations too, and leaves other motion alone)")).
- New: content that scrolls sideways shows that it scrolls. A ScrollArea (and so a Table), a CodeBlock and a Chart's visible table, while their content is wider than they are, fade it out at the side where it goes on, following the scroll and resizing. A ScrollArea's focus ring is now drawn on its root, and a CodeBlock's on an element over its code, so the fade leaves them whole; the ScrollArea's scrollbar thumb is `CanvasText` in forced colors.
- Fixed: under forced colours, the pressed SegmentedControl segment keeps its HighlightText letters while the pointer rests on it, where hover used to turn them back to the page's text colour on the Highlight thumb.
- New: Toaster's `avoid` takes a CSS selector for controls the toasts must stay clear of, such as a call's Join button in a corner: while one is in the lower half of the window under the toasts' column, [the toasts sit 8px above it, and come back down when it goes](registry/ouro/components/ouro/toast/toaster.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/toast/toaster.test.tsx::Toaster avoid > keeps the toasts clear of a control the selector matches under them, and comes back down when it goes)").
