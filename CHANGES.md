# Changes in this release

- New: a theme can give the solid primary `Button`, the page's main action, its own colours with `--ouro-action` and `--ouro-action-foreground`, while soft, outline and plain primary Buttons, `Link`, checkboxes, switches, radios, toggles and the current tab keep `--primary` (for a link in the action colour, use `<Button href>`); unset, they fall back to `--primary` and `--primary-foreground`, so a theme that sets neither looks as it did ([#124](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/issues/124 "verify:testExists(test/action-token.test.tsx::the action token > repaints the solid primary Button, as a button and as a link, and leaves every selection on --primary)")).
- Site: the Cookbook preset sets `--ouro-action` to Spoonjoy's charcoal action (amber in the dark), so its primary buttons are charcoal and its selection stays brass ([themes.mts](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/site/scripts/themes.mts "verify:fileLine(site/scripts/themes.mts, /--ouro-action: var\(--sj-action\)/)")).
- New: `AttentionCard`, a card for something that waits on the person (a mention, a question, an unanswered request): a badge says why, then where, when, who and what they wrote, with an optional open button and actions; [screen readers hear it by its reason and where it happened](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/attention-card/attention-card.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/attention-card/attention-card.test.tsx::AttentionCard > is an article named by its reason and where it happened)").
- New: `ConversationList`, a list of conversations to choose from: [a row shows the avatar, title, time and latest message, and unread rows are bold with a dot](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/conversation-list/conversation-list.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/conversation-list/conversation-list.test.tsx::ConversationList > shows unread conversations with a bold title, a dot and a spoken label)"); the open row is marked current, and [arrow keys, Home and End move between rows](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/conversation-list/conversation-list.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/conversation-list/conversation-list.test.tsx::ConversationList > moves focus with the arrow keys, Home and End)").
- New: `ImageField` takes a dropped photo: it says so beside the choose button ("or drop a photo here", `dropHint`), a file dragged over the field lights the photo's frame and says where to drop it (`dropLabel`) while text or a link dragged over leaves it unlit, and a dropped file goes through the same type and size checks, cropper and messages as one chosen with the button ([#186](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/issues/186 "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/image-field/image-field.test.tsx::ImageField dropping a file > lights the frame while a file is over the field, and takes a dropped photo as if chosen) verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/image-field/image-field.test.tsx::ImageField dropping a file > says beside the choose button that a photo can be dropped, in the app's words, and not while disabled) verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/image-field/image-field.test.tsx::ImageField dropping a file > turns a dropped file away by type and size with the picker's messages, and crops a dropped photo with crop)")).
- `Item` with `href`: the row's link is described by its `description` (`aria-describedby`), so a screen reader that lands on a result hears its title and then its byline and snippet; the layout is unchanged ([#183](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/issues/183 "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/item/item.test.tsx::Item with href > describes the row's link by its description, which keeps its place under the title) verify:fileLine(registry/ouro/e2e/screen-reader/item.sr.spec.ts, /is heard as a link by its title, then its description/)")).
- New: `Link` takes `reloadDocument`, which renders a plain `<a>` for an internal path too, skipping `OuroProvider`'s `linkComponent`, for paths your router has no page for (a resource route, a file download, a sign-in that starts on the server); a link with `download` does the same ([#184](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/issues/184 "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/link/link.test.tsx::Link > renders a plain <a> with reloadDocument or download, skipping the router's link, in the same look)")).
- Menu: [`modal={false}` leaves the page scrolling and live while a dropdown is open](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/menu/menu.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/menu/menu.test.tsx::Menu modal > with modal={false}, leaves the page's scroll alone while a modal Menu's lock comes and goes)"), which spares the browser a layout and repaint of the whole page as the menu opens and closes; the phone sheet still holds the page. [Reorderable's menu of moves leaves the page's scroll alone](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/reorderable/reorderable.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/reorderable/reorderable.test.tsx::Reorderable > from the handle's menu, with no drag > leaves the page's scroll alone while the menu is open: its menu does not hold the page)"). [A context menu and a menu in a MenuBar warn that they do not use `modal`](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/menu/menu.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/menu/menu.test.tsx::Menu modal > warns that a context menu does not use modal, and drops it)").
- New: `ReactionBar`, a message's reactions as [toggle buttons named by label and count](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/reaction-bar/reaction-bar.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/reaction-bar/reaction-bar.test.tsx::ReactionBar > shows each reaction as a toggle button named by its label and count)"), the person's own pressed and tinted, and quick reactions to add with a single press; [read-only without `onReact`](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/reaction-bar/reaction-bar.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/reaction-bar/reaction-bar.test.tsx::ReactionBar > is read-only without onReact, and says which reaction is yours)").
- New: `Reorderable` takes `disabled`, which stops drag, the menu of moves and keyboard pick-up (for example while a save is in flight), keeps the handles focusable and marked `aria-disabled`, and puts back a row held or dragged when it turns on ([#185](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/issues/185 "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/reorderable/reorderable.test.tsx::Reorderable > disabled > takes no drag, no menu and no pick-up, and keeps its handles focusable and marked unavailable) verify:fileLine(registry/ouro/e2e/screen-reader/reorderable.sr.spec.ts, /heard as unavailable buttons, without the pick-up instructions/)")).
- Tabs: under forced colours (Windows contrast themes) a tab draws a border, so it keeps an edge when its fill and the underline drop away.
- Tabs: under forced colours the selected tab is filled with Highlight and lettered in HighlightText, so it stands out from the others.
- New: `Tabs` takes links for views of one page that each have a URL (a section's pages, a search's scopes): items with `href` render a `<nav>` of links in the tabs' look, through your router's link, with the current one marked `aria-current="page"` from `currentPath` (by its query when the tabs share a path) or `value`, in a row or a column, and Tab moves between them as between any links ([#128](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/issues/128 "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/tabs/tabs.test.tsx::Tabs as links > renders a named nav of links, marking the current page, with the tabs' look) verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/tabs/tabs.test.tsx::Tabs as links > marks the tab for the current path, by its query when the tabs share a path) verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/tabs/tabs.test.tsx::Tabs as links > moves between the links with Tab, and leaves the arrow keys to the page)")).
- Fixed: [in a right-to-left page, vertical `Tabs` draw their hairline on the left, the column's end edge, where the indicator already sits](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/blob/main/registry/ouro/components/ouro/tabs/tabs.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/tabs/tabs.test.tsx::Tabs as links > keeps the hairline and the current line together on the left of a vertical column in a right-to-left page)"), with panels and with links.
- New: `Timer` and `TimerTray` take `alert="repeat"`, which chimes and vibrates again every 2.5 seconds until the timer is dismissed, restarted or cancelled in any of its views, its last view leaves the page, or `repeatFor` seconds pass (two minutes by default, also for a `repeatFor` that is not finite and above 0); screen readers still hear "Time's up" once, and `silent` keeps it quiet ([#179](https://github.com/ourostack/ouro-ui/issues/179 "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/timer/timer.test.tsx::Timer > chimes and buzzes again every 2.5 seconds with alert=repeat, says it once, and stops on Dismiss) verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/timer/timer.test.tsx::Timer > keeps a repeating alert going for two minutes by default) verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/timer/timer.test.tsx::Timer > stops a repeating alert when the last view of its timer goes) verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/timer/timer-tray.test.tsx::TimerTray > stops its repeating alert when the timer's row leaves the tray, and when the tray goes)")).
