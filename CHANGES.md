# Changes in this release

- Fixed: [Command with `recents` and nothing typed](registry/ouro/components/ouro/command/command.recents.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/command/command.recents.test.tsx::Command recents > says nothing of an empty list while the recents show with nothing typed, and says it once something typed matches nothing)") draws no empty label under the chips and announces none, and the label comes back once a search matches nothing.
- Changed: Composer's expand and collapse no longer animates its height, which made the browser lay the page out while it ran: the box takes its new height at once and a clip over it opens or closes, with the toolbar fading and sliding, and with reduced motion the toolbar only fades; the frame carries `data-ouro-moving` and a bubbling `ouro-composer-move` event gives the heights it moves between, so add `ouro-base` again to get its keyframes.
- New: [Lightbox takes `fit`](registry/ouro/components/ouro/lightbox/lightbox.fit.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/lightbox/lightbox.fit.test.tsx::Lightbox fit > draws no panel, and a press beside the image dismisses it, but not one on the image, the caption or a button)"), which shows the picture alone, centred and fitted with no panel, dismissed by a press beside it, growing from the thumbnail's size, with quiet buttons.
- Fix: Ouro check's `near-miss` rule no longer flags a surface sitting a few pixels inside the edge of a button or a switch, checkbox, radio or slider role, since a control's own parts (Switch's track and thumb, a custom checkbox's box and mark) are drawn as one piece.
- Fixed: SnapPiP announces when its picture is put away and brought back, as the Hide and Show buttons and an automatic hide change it.
