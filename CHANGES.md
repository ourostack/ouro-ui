# Changes in this release

- New: `Accordion`, with `items` and `multiple` (which makes `value` a list), `headingLevel` for the titles' headings, panels that grow to their measured height with CSS, and closed content that the browser's page search finds and opens.
- New: `AspectRatio`, a frame that keeps an image or video at `ratio` (a number such as `16 / 9`) while its width follows the layout.
- New: `Attachment`, a file on a chat message: its icon, name and size (`formatSize`), a link with `href`, and a remove button with `onRemove`, named by `removeLabel`.
- `Button` and `Toggle` now set `data-variant` to their look (`solid`, `soft`, `outline`, `plain`), so a group or an app style can select on it; `ButtonGroup` uses it to draw separators between borderless buttons.
- New: `ButtonGroup`, which joins `Button`s and `Toggle`s into one named row: only the outer corners are round, outline borders are shared, and borderless buttons get a separator.
- New: `Calendar` and `DatePicker`. `Calendar` is an ARIA grid with one Tab stop (arrows, Home and End, Page Up and Page Down, Shift for years) that picks one day or, with `range`, a start and an end; `min`, `max` and `isDateDisabled` make days unavailable, and `locale` sets the names, digits and first day of the week through `Intl`. `DatePicker` is a field on the `Field` contract: a text box for typing the date with a button that opens the calendar on desktops, and the phone's own `<input type="date">` on touch screens; both submit the ISO date (`2026-10-11`) under `name`. Every button label and the invalid-date message are props with English defaults.
- New: `Card`, a surface with `title`, `description`, `action`, `media`, a body and `footer`. With `href` the whole card is one link with one tab stop, named by its title, and every control inside (a share button over the cover, a link in the body, a button in `action` or `footer`) stays its own tab stop and takes its own clicks and taps. `stackBelow="sm"` drops the action under the title on a phone.
- `Carousel` in a browser without the `scrollend` event (older Safari) reports and announces a change only once the scroll has held still for two frames and, after a Previous or Next press, has reached that press's slide. A busy page could make it report the slide it was passing, then the one it stopped on.
- New: `Carousel`, built on the browser's own scroll snapping (no Embla): each child is a slide named "Slide 2 of 5", Previous and Next buttons with 44px touch targets, `loop`, `slidesPerView`, `defaultIndex` and `onIndexChange`, one polite announcement per position change once the scroll ends (never while a finger is held still mid-swipe), slides out of view made `inert`, focus kept off the page when a button becomes disabled, no smooth scrolling under reduced motion, and a vertical swipe on the slides still scrolls the page. Every visible and spoken string is a prop with an English default: `previousLabel`, `nextLabel`, `slideLabel`, and the role descriptions `roleDescription` ("carousel") and `slideRoleDescription` ("slide").
- New: `Chart`, theme-token glue over recharts (a new dependency of the `chart` item). Pass `title`, `data`, `categoryKey` and a `config` that maps each series to a label and a `tone` (`chart-1` to `chart-5`, or `primary`, `destructive`, `success`, `warning`), and `type` draws bars, lines or areas; or pass a recharts chart as children with `<ChartTooltip />` and `<ChartLegend />`, as in shadcn (`ChartContainer` is the same component). The chart is a figure named by its title, with a summary and a table of every value for screen readers (`showTable` shows it), a plot whose tooltip the arrow keys move, and a marker shape per series in the legend, so color is never the only cue. The tooltip is a polite live region, so screen readers hear each point the arrow keys reach. recharts adds about 120 KB gzip to the page that renders a chart.
- New: `Checkbox`, `CheckboxGroup`, `Switch` and `RadioGroup` (with `Radio`), on the `Field` contract. `Checkbox` takes `checked`, `onCheckedChange` and `indeterminate` (announced as mixed), and its tick draws itself. `CheckboxGroup` and `RadioGroup` take `legend` and their choices as `options` or as `Checkbox`/`Radio` children; `CheckboxGroup`'s `required` blocks submit until a box is checked, with `requiredMessage` to translate the browser's message, and `RadioGroup` takes `orientation`. `Switch`'s thumb stretches toward its travel while pressed. Each control is named and described in the server HTML, keeps 44px touch targets and 16px labels on touch screens, and its label is to the right of the control.
- `Field` lays out a checkbox, switch or radio with the control first and the label to its right, and names a group with a legend; the field context now carries the label id and the invalid state.
- New: `Collapsible`, one section that opens under its `title`.
- New: `ColorField`, a native color swatch and a hex text input kept in step on the `Field` contract; it takes short or long hex, always submits `#rrggbb`, goes back to `defaultValue` on form reset, and shows a plain swatch beside the hex text where the browser has no color picker.
- New: `Combobox`, a text input that filters `items` as people type, on the `Field` contract. It picks one item, several as chips with `multiple`, or any text with `allowCustomValue`; `onInputChange` and `loading` search on your server, and "No results" waits until something is typed. On a phone the field is a button that opens a full-height bottom sheet with the search input pinned at the top, with a clear button beside it; with `allowCustomValue` the sheet's first row keeps the typed text. `loadingLabel`, `emptyLabel`, `clearLabel`, `doneLabel`, `removeLabel` and `customValueLabel` translate its text.
- New: `DescriptionList`, one thing's details as `items` of `{ term, details }`. On a phone each term sits above its details; from 640px (`stackBelow="md"`: 768px) the terms form a column beside them, with a line between the pairs.
- New: `Empty`, the empty state for a list, search or page, with `icon`, `title`, `description` and `action`.
- New: `FileField`, a native file input in a drop zone on the `Field` contract, with a list of the chosen files (image thumbnails, remove buttons named with `removeLabel`), `accept`, `multiple` and `maxSize` filtering, a message under the field for each file left out, announced as a status and shown next to the field's errors (translatable with `rejectMessage`), and `onReject` reporting them.
- New: `Form`, a `<form>` that validates with the browser's own constraints and takes `errors` by field name (`errors={actionData?.errors}`). Each message shows on the field with that `name`, linked through `aria-describedby` in the server HTML too, and when errors arrive focus moves to the first field with one. The `form` key shows in an alert at the top of the form, first in the DOM and in the server HTML. A key that no rendered field is named (a typo, a field in a closed panel) joins it once the page hydrates. No message shows twice. Server errors only show: they never block the next submit. A Form's submit stops at that Form, so a Form in a sheet never submits the Form the sheet sits in. `action` and `method` pass through, and `render={<RouterForm />}` submits through React Router.
- New: `EditSheet`, a `Dialog` with a `Form` inside (a bottom sheet on phones): `onSubmit` gets the fields as `FormData` and may return errors or a promise; the save button shows `loading` while it runs and the sheet cannot be dismissed (its close button is disabled), errors land on their fields, a failed save shows `failureMessage`, and success closes it and returns focus to the trigger. Pressing Save or Cancel leaves focus in the field, so on a phone the keyboard stays up and the tap lands. Dismissing it after an edit, including after a save that failed, asks inside the sheet first (`discardMessage`, `discardLabel`, `keepEditingLabel`); `onDiscard` runs when the user discards, so an app resets its controlled fields there.
- `Field` (and every control built on it) shows a `Form`'s error for its `name` when it has no `error` of its own, and drops it once the user changes the control. A Field's `error` now only shows: it no longer marks the control invalid to Base UI, so it never blocks a form's next submit (the browser's own checks still do). The label now has an id of its own (`<id>-label`).
- New: `Item` and `ItemGroup`. `Item` is a list row with `media`, `title`, `description` and `actions`; with `href` the whole row is one link, at least 44px tall on a touch screen, and every control inside stays its own tab stop and takes its own clicks and taps. `stackBelow="sm"` drops the actions under the text on a phone, and `mediaAbove="sm"` puts the photo full width above the text there, as a recipe list does. `ItemGroup` makes the rows a list named by its `label`, with lines between them and an `empty` message.
- New: `MessageList`, a labeled, scrolling conversation that opens at the newest message and follows new messages only while the reader is at the bottom; once they scroll up their view stays put and a "Jump to latest" button (`jumpLabel`) appears. Each new message from someone else is announced once, politely (`newMessageLabel`), and a streamed reply when it finishes. History never is: not the first messages to fill the list, not older messages loaded above, and not a message whose key changes in place. Older messages loaded above a scrolled-up reader leave their view where it was.
- New: `Message`, one chat message with `role` (`user`, `assistant`, `system`), a required author `name`, an `avatar` (Avatar's initials without a picture), `attachments`, and `streaming`, which marks a reply still being written.
- Changed: motion tokens. New `--ouro-ease-out`, `--ouro-ease-in-out`, `--ouro-ease-drawer`, `--ouro-duration-press` and `--ouro-duration-sheet`; `--ouro-duration-fast` is now 150ms (was 100ms) and `--ouro-duration-slow` 300ms (was 400ms), so dialogs, backdrops and anything else on those tokens move at the new lengths; color changes on pressable controls now ease with `ease`; the `press` utility scales in and out in 160ms instead of a 750ms spring, and a Tabs tab scales its label and icon, not the tab, so the indicator stays on the tab; popovers, hover cards and tooltips open on the ease-out token (200ms, tooltips 150ms) and no longer use `ease-in`; an app that overrode `--ouro-spring-duration` or `--ouro-ease-spring` to tune presses or popups no longer changes them (the tokens stay defined for now); a linked Item, Card or Table row highlights on hover only for a fine pointer.
- New: `NumberField`, a number input on the `Field` contract. It formats with `format` and `locale` (currency, percent, units), steps with its Decrease and Increase buttons (44px touch hit areas) and the arrow keys within `min` and `max`, and scrubs when the label is dragged sideways with a mouse. It picks the phone keyboard itself: a decimal keypad for fractions and a digit keypad for whole numbers when `min` is 0 or more, and the full keyboard when negatives are allowed, since iOS keypads have no minus key. `decrementLabel` and `incrementLabel` translate the button names.
- New: `OtpField`, one-time code entry on the `Field` contract, built on Base UI OTP Field: one slot per character (`length`, default 6), paste of a whole code, `autoComplete="one-time-code"`, `validationType`, `groupSize`, `autoSubmit` and `slotLabel` to translate the slot names.
- The `ouro-testing` kit adds `rotate(width, height)` (testing/rotate.ts), which resizes the viewport and then resets the page zoom that Chromium's phone emulation can leave after a rotation, and the `pageZoom`, `resetPageZoom` and `setPageZoom` commands. `rotate` throws, naming the test, if something else had zoomed the page, after still resizing and resetting the zoom. The `ouro-touch` project now fails any test that leaves the page zoomed, naming it.
- New: `Resizable` and `ResizablePanel`, with `ResizablePanelGroup` and `ResizableHandle` as parts: panels sized in percent with window-splitter handles (`role="separator"`) that work with a mouse, touch (a 44px hit area) and the keyboard (arrow keys, Home, End, Enter), and `autoSaveId`, which keeps the layout in localStorage and ignores storage that is unavailable.
- New: `ScrollArea`, a scrolling region on Base UI with a slim themed scrollbar. It keeps native scrolling, and on a touch screen the scrollbar never takes a touch. It hands the scroll on to the page at its end unless you set `contain`, and warns in development when it becomes a tab stop with no `label`.
- New: `SegmentedControl`, an iOS-style control on Base UI's ToggleGroup in single mode for switching a view in place: equal segments with a raised thumb that slides to the checked one, `label` with `hideLabel`, `items`, `fullWidth`, and exactly one segment checked. Screen readers hear a radio group, the arrow keys check the next segment as they do for radio buttons, and `name` submits the value with a form.
- New: `Select`, on Base UI Select (Catalyst's Listbox), with `options` or `SelectItem` and `SelectGroup` children, groups, `placeholder`, and `multiple`, which makes the value a `string[]`. Below `sm` on a coarse pointer its list opens as a bottom sheet. Its popup stacks at `--ouro-layer` plus 1000, so it opens above a Dialog or Sheet it sits in.
- New: `NativeSelect`, a styled native `<select>` on the `Field` contract, with `options` (and groups) or `<option>` children and a `placeholder`.
- New: `ShowMore`, which cuts a list to `initialCount` items, clamps text to `lines` lines, or grows a list a page at a time with `onShowMore`, `hasMore`, `loading` and a no-JavaScript `href`. "Show more" moves focus to the first newly shown item, a list that loads more says how many items now show (`loadedLabel`) or that there are no more (`noMoreLabel`), and `showMoreLabel` and `showLessLabel` translate the button.
- New: `Slider`, on the `Field` contract. It takes one value, or an array for a range with a thumb per value; it names each thumb after the label ("Minimum, Price", translated with `thumbLabels`), and shows the formatted value with `showValue`. Its thumbs keep a 44px hit area on touch screens, and a vertical finger drag over it scrolls the page.
- New: `Stack` and `Row`, layout-only primitives with `gap` and `align` (and `justify`, Row's `wrap` and `stackBelow`), so agents stop hand-writing flex classes. A `gap-*`, `items-*`, `justify-*`, `flex-row`, `flex-col` or `flex-wrap` class on either warns in development with the prop to use.
- New: `Table`, a data table from `columns` and `rows` (or from `TableHeader`, `TableBody`, `TableFooter`, `TableRow`, `TableHead` and `TableCell` parts), named by a required `caption` (`hideCaption`). It scrolls sideways inside its own region on a phone, and `stickyHeader` keeps the header at the top inside a height you set, while it also scrolls sideways. With `rowHref` (a function, or the name of a field, which a Server Component can pass) each row is one link with one tab stop, named by its row header (the column with `rowHeader`, or else the first without a `cell` renderer, so a leading checkbox column never holds the link) and at least 44px tall on a touch screen, and buttons, links and inputs in its cells keep their own taps; `rowExternal` opens row links in a new tab, announced with `externalLabel`. `striped`, `dense`, a column's `align` and `wrap`, and an `empty` message ("Nothing here yet." by default). It does not sort or page.
- `Item` and `Card` now share the whole-row link with `Table` (`lib/ouro/stretched-link.ts`): a linked root carries `data-linked` and its link `data-stretched-link`, and a linked `Item` or `Card` inside another linked `Item`, `Card` or table row keeps its own link.
- New: `Tabs`, with `items` (each `{ value, label, content }`) or the parts in `tabs-parts` (`TabsRoot`, `TabsList`, `TabsTab`, `TabsIndicator`, `TabsPanel`), `orientation` (`horizontal` or `vertical`) and `activation` (`automatic` or `manual`). A line slides to the selected tab with the theme spring, tabs are 44px tall on touch screens, a row that scrolls fades out on the side that hides more tabs, vertical tabs become a row on a phone, and the selected tab and its panel name each other in server HTML.
- `openStory` (the e2e helper in `e2e/ouro/story.ts`) also waits for the app's web fonts to load, so a spec that measures no longer sees text change width when a font such as a shadcn preset's Inter arrives late.
- The `ouro-touch` test project turns touch emulation back on before every test, through the new `commands.restoreTouch()`. Chromium drops it after a screenshot taller than the viewport, such as the one Vitest takes when a test fails on a tall page, so one failing touch test no longer fails every later test that checks for a coarse pointer.
- `commands.touchDrag` takes `durationMs`, the time from touch down to lift as the page's event timestamps report it. Pass it when the result depends on the swipe's speed, as over scroll snap points, where a fast flick carries past the next slide.
- The test kit has `commands.touchStep({ type, x, y })`, one step of a real one-finger touch gesture (Chromium only), for a finger held still mid-swipe. A finger a test leaves down is lifted before the next `touchStep` or `touchDrag` gesture starts.
- New: `ToggleGroup`, a labeled row of toggles from `items` with arrow-key focus; one at a time by default (`value` is a string or `null`), several with `multiple` (`value` is a list).
- New: `Toggle`, a button that stays pressed (`pressed`, `defaultPressed`, `onPressedChange`) with one name (`label`, `hideLabel` with an `icon` for icon-only) and `aria-pressed`, in `plain` and `outline` variants and Button's sizes.
