# Changes in this release

- Fixed: A Button that starts loading [draws its spinner before its first style pass, even when passive effects have run](registry/ouro/components/ouro/button/button.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/button/button.test.tsx::loading > draws the spinner at once when the button mounts loading, and fades it in when loading turns on later)").
- Fixed: Composer [measures its initial frame on the browser's animation pass rather than during the mount commit](registry/ouro/components/ouro/composer/composer.first-height.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/composer/composer.first-height.test.tsx::Composer first height > reads the frame in its first animation pass, not during the mount commit)"), and a [quote present at mount draws without an entrance transition](registry/ouro/components/ouro/composer/composer.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/composer/composer.test.tsx::Composer quote > draws a quote present at mount at once, and fades one in that arrives later)").
- MediaTile: [report `(false, 0, 0)` to the latest visibility callback on unmount](registry/ouro/components/ouro/media-tile/media-tile.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/media-tile/media-tile.test.tsx::MediaTile visibility > stops watching when it unmounts)") so an app can stop receiving the picture.
- Toolbar: use an item's [`tone`](registry/ouro/components/ouro/toolbar/toolbar.floating.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/toolbar/toolbar.floating.test.tsx::Toolbar floating > uses item tones for pressed toggles and state-named buttons, in %s)") for a filled button or a pressed toggle: `inverse`, `primary` or `destructive`; a destructive action [keeps its tone in More](registry/ouro/components/ouro/toolbar/toolbar.floating.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/toolbar/toolbar.floating.test.tsx::Toolbar floating > keeps a destructive trailing action destructive in More)").
