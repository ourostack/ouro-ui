# Changes in this release

- New: [`disabledReason` on Button, Toggle, ToggleGroup items, Switch, Checkbox, Radio, Tabs, SegmentedControl, MenuItem, Select, Toolbar entries and Accordion items](registry/ouro/components/ouro/button/button.reason.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/button/button.reason.test.tsx::Button disabledReason > turns the button off with aria-disabled, keeps it focusable and describes it by the reason)") turns the control off with `aria-disabled`, keeps it focusable, ties the reason to it with `aria-describedby` and shows it as a tooltip on hover and focus; ReactionBar and Reorderable take `disabledReason` for the whole control, Calendar and DatePicker take `dateDisabledReason`, and Ouro check's `disabled-no-reason` rule passes a busy control (`aria-busy`), such as a loading Button.
- Added: `Card` takes `backdrop`, a picture behind the whole card with its title, body and footer over it. [Only the picture is clipped to the card's corner; the card and its text and controls aren't](registry/ouro/components/ouro/card/card.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/card/card.test.tsx::Card > lays a backdrop behind the whole card, edge to edge, clipping only the picture and not the card or its text)"), and where the engine has no corner-shape [the picture takes the avatar's squircle clip at the card's radius](registry/ouro/theme/squircle-surface.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/theme/squircle-surface.test.tsx::squircle surface where the engine has no corner-shape > clips a Card's backdrop, and only it, to the squircle at the card's radius)").
