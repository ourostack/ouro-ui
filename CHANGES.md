# Changes in this release

- New: `Sheet` takes `closeOnOutsidePress`, so a panel opened with `modal={false}` [closes on a press on the page](registry/ouro/components/ouro/sheet/sheet.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/sheet/sheet.test.tsx::non-modal sheet > with closeOnOutsidePress, closes on a press on the page, and the press still reaches the page)"), and [when focus moves out of it](registry/ouro/components/ouro/sheet/sheet.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/sheet/sheet.test.tsx::non-modal sheet > with closeOnOutsidePress, closes when focus moves out of it by %s, and leaves focus where it went)"), while [menus and dialogs opened from inside it stay part of it](registry/ouro/components/ouro/sheet/sheet.test.tsx "verify:testExists(registry/ouro/components/ouro/sheet/sheet.test.tsx::non-modal sheet > with closeOnOutsidePress, stays open on a press in a menu or a dialog opened from inside it)"). Give-back from an app that shows a record's details in a side panel beside its list, which had built this close itself.
